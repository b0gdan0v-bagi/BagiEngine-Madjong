# Mahjong Module Guidelines

## Overview

`MahjongGame` is an `IGame` (name `Mahjong`, scene `MahjongScene`). Executable `BagiMahjong`, module `MahjongModule`, tests `MahjongTests`. Namespace `BECore::Mahjong`. Infrastructure and how a game is registered: `engine/src/Modules/Games/CLAUDE.md`; how this repository was set up around the engine: `engine/NEW_GAME.md` (on the engine's `main`).

**State: stages 1-2 of the rules exist and stage 3 (scoring and the match) is in progress.** The game is registered, starts in Game mode (`mode="Game"` in `config/ApplicationConfig.xml`; set `mode="Editor"` to open it in the scene editor) and shows its scene with a title. `Game/` holds the rules of classic Chinese mahjong for four seats as plain C++, from the tiles to a finished hand: the tile set, the seeded RNG, the seats, the wall, the deal, the hand shape and `HandModel` (turns, claims, the three kongs, robbing, winning, the drawn hand, the list of legal actions). Scoring is being built (`Score.h`: a seat's points, the doubles for everyone, the winner's lines and the limit hands, see "Scoring"); payments and the match (hands, the move of the winds), the computer players, the control API and the scene (components, assets, HUD) are next; `MahjongGame` does not use the model yet.

## Wall conventions

The break, the loose tiles and the dead wall are assumptions of this repository (`Wall.h` has the details; the numbers are named constants). The ring is four sides of 17 stacks of two tiles (18 stacks with flowers). East rolls 2d6 `a`: side `(a - 1) % 4` counted from East's own wall is broken; the owner of that side rolls `b`; the break is after `a + b` stacks from the right end of that side. The sides lie clockwise in the ring, so a count that runs past the left end of a side goes on into the wall of the clockwise neighbour, as on a real table, and `GetBreak().index` is the physical position of the break. Drawing starts at the break. The first 2 tiles are the loose reserve (a loose draw is refilled from the live tail), the last 14 are the dead wall and are never drawn.

## HandModel

`Game/HandModel.h` plays one hand; the wall is built outside (`Begin(settings, wall, seatWinds, roundWind)` deals and gives East the first tile). The phase (`Phase`) is `Discard` (the current seat holds one tile too many and acts), `Claim` (a discard is pending and the other seats decide), `RobKong` (an upgraded kong is pending, the other seats may only claim mahjong) or `Finished`. A draw is automatic: every transition that ends in a draw performs it (`Receive`), so a seat only sees a phase it can act in. A drawn bonus tile is laid open and replaced from the loose reserve, again and again; no tile to draw (an empty live wall when a draw is due, an empty reserve when a replacement is due) ends the hand as `ResultKind::Drawn`. Actions (`Discard(seat, tile, declareCalling)`, `DeclareWin(seat)`) return `std::expected<void, HandError>`; a refused action changes nothing. Nothing is compulsory: a seat that could win discards instead if it wants. The calling hand is declared with a discard (`declareCalling`): the 13 tiles that stay must be waiting (`WinningTiles(PlayerHand)`, the copies in the seat's melds counted); `callingAtDiscardCount` is the number of earlier discards of the seat, so "declared in the first hand" is `== 0`; the declaration is not binding afterwards. A self-draw win (`DeclareWin`) needs the declaration and a winning shape and ends the hand with `HandResult { Win, winner, winningTile, WinSource::SelfDraw, from, loose, lastLiveTile }`: `loose` when the tile came from the loose reserve, `lastLiveTile` only for a self-drawn win on the last tile drawn from the live wall (not for a win on a discard, on a loose tile even when its refill emptied the live wall, or by robbing a kong). The shared pile is `Discards()`. Claims: a discard opens `Phase::Claim` only when some other seat has a legal claim (`LegalClaims(seat)`: `ClaimOption { ClaimKind Chow | Pung | Kong | Mahjong, chowFirst }`); otherwise the next seat draws at once. A seat in the window (`PendingClaimSeats()`, `GetClaimStatus`) decides once: `Claim(seat, kind, chowFirst)` or `Pass(seat)` (always allowed; a second decision is `AlreadyDecided`, a seat outside the window `NotYourTurn`, a claim it cannot make `IllegalClaim`). A chow only for the seat after the discarder (`chowFirst` is the lowest tile of the chosen sequence, a seat may have several; never honours, never wrapping); a pung needs two equal tiles, a kong three; mahjong needs the calling-hand declaration (`NotCalling`) and a winning shape with the tile (`CannotWin`). When every seat in the window has decided the best claim wins: mahjong, then pung or kong, then chow; two mahjong claims go to the seat farther counter-clockwise from the discarder (`Offset` larger). The claimed tile leaves the pile and goes into an open `Meld` (`fromDiscard`, `from`, `claimed`); the claimer then discards without drawing (`DeclareWin` is refused: no drawn tile), after a kong it first draws a loose tile, and the turn after its discard goes to the seat after the claimer (seats are skipped). All pass: the seat after the discarder draws. A mahjong claim ends the hand with `WinSource::Discard`, `from` the discarder, the tile joins the winner's concealed tiles. In `Claim` `CurrentSeat()` is the discarder and the pending tile is `Discards().back()`. Kongs of the own turn: `DeclareConcealedKong(seat, tile)` turns four equal concealed tiles into a concealed `Meld` (four tiles left in the hand are no kong; the declaration is never compulsory), `UpgradeKong(seat, tile)` adds the fourth tile to an open pung (the meld turns into a `Kong`) but only with the tile drawn from the wall this turn (a regular or a loose draw: not a copy kept from earlier turns, not right after a claim); both are refused with `IllegalKong` when the hand does not allow them, and both draw a loose tile (`Receive(seat, DrawLoose(), true)`: a bonus tile is replaced again, an empty reserve ends the hand as drawn) before the seat discards. An upgraded kong can be robbed, only to win: when another seat has declared a calling hand that the tile completes, `Phase::RobKong` opens (`CurrentSeat()` is the kong's owner, `PendingTile()` the tile), those seats `Claim(seat, ClaimKind::Mahjong)` or `Pass`, the farther one counter-clockwise from the owner wins, the hand ends with `WinSource::RobbedKong` and `from` the owner, the tile is taken from the kong (the owner keeps a pung); if nobody robs, the kong is completed. A win on a tile from the reserve has `loose` (after a kong or a bonus replacement) and never `lastLiveTile`. `AfterDiscard` decides what follows a discard and `Receive` gives a seat a tile. `PendingTile()` is valid only while a window is open (it is reset when the window closes).

Actions as data (the surface of the computer player and the control API): `HandAction { ActionKind Discard | DeclareWin | ConcealedKong | UpgradeKong | Claim | Pass, tile, declareCalling, claim, chowFirst }` with the factories `MakeDiscard`, `MakeDeclareWin`, `MakeConcealedKong`, `MakeUpgradeKong`, `MakeClaim`, `MakePass`. `LegalActions(seat)` lists what the seat can do now: on its turn a `Discard` of each distinct concealed tile (and the same with `declareCalling` where the rest is waiting), `DeclareWin`, a `ConcealedKong` for each tile held four times, an `UpgradeKong` for the open pung of the tile drawn this turn; in `Claim` / `RobKong` the `LegalClaims` plus `Pass` for a seat that has not decided; empty for every other seat, after the end, and for a number that is no seat. `Apply(seat, action)` dispatches to the method of the kind; an action that is not in the list is refused with that method's error and changes nothing. `Claim` asks first whether the seat is in the window (`NotYourTurn`), only then about the declaration (`NotCalling` / `CannotWin`). `Begin` returns `BadSeatWinds` (and changes nothing) unless the winds hold East exactly once; a model that was never begun is a finished, drawn hand, so a result exists exactly when the phase is `Finished`. `GetHand` and `SeatWind` take a real seat (`IsSeat`); `GetClaimStatus` answers `NotInWindow` for any other number.

## Scoring

`Game/Score.h`: scoring is a pure function, `ScoreSeat(const ScoreInput&)`, so a test builds a hand from notation without a `HandModel`. `ScoreInput { hand, seatWind, roundWind, limit, won, result, visibleWinningTileCopies }` (for the winner the concealed tiles include the winning tile; `result` is read only when `won`). The answer is a `ScoreBreakdown { won, lines, points, doubles, raw, limitHand, score }`: `lines` are `ScoreLine { ScoreKind kind, tile, points, doubles }` of the best reading in a stable order (points: sets, pair, bonus tiles; then doubles); `points` and `doubles` are their sums, `raw` is `points << doubles` (64-bit inside, saturating at `INT_MAX`), `score` is `RoundUpToTen(min(raw, LimitCap(limit)))`, where `LimitCap` is the limit rounded down to a multiple of 10 (0 for a negative one), so a score is a multiple of 10 and never above the limit, also for a limit that is not a multiple of 10 or is `INT_MAX`. The lines run: the winner's points (`Win`, `WinTileFromWall`, `OnlyWinningTile`), the sets, the pair, the bonus tiles, the doubles for everyone, the doubles for the winner. Every number of the rules is a named constant in `Score.h`.

**Readings** (`ScoreReading.h`): a hand is read as sets and a pair before it is scored, and the reading with the largest `raw` wins (a tie keeps the first); all lines of a breakdown come from one reading. The winner: the melds are sets as they are, the concealed tiles go through `Decompose` and each decomposition is a candidate; when the win came from a discard or a robbed kong the set that holds the winning tile is a claimed (open) set, so each place the tile may sit is a candidate of its own (a self-draw: one per decomposition; the winning tile as the pair opens nothing). Four equal concealed tiles never declared a kong read as a concealed pung. A winner whose hand has no four-sets-and-a-pair reading and is no limit hand (seven pairs and thirteen orphans are limit hands, below; or a test input that is no win at all) is read like a seat that does not win (it gets the winner's points and the circumstance doubles). A seat that does not win: the melds as they are, every kind held three or four times in the concealed tiles one concealed pung, and of the kinds held exactly twice the best scoring pair; chows in the concealed tiles are not looked for.

**Lines so far:** a pung or kong scores 2 (simples, open) times 2 for terminals, winds and dragons, times 2 when concealed, times 4 for a kong; a pair of dragons, of the own wind and of the round wind 2 each (own and round at once 4); a bonus tile 4; a chow 0. Doubles for everyone: a pung or kong of dragons, of the own wind, of the round wind 1 each (`DragonSet`, `OwnWindSet`, `RoundWindSet`); one suit with honours 1, without honours 3 (read on all tiles of the seat, the melds' too; bonus tiles are not looked at); a flower or season whose number is the own wind's or the round wind's 1 each (`OwnBonus`, `RoundBonus`; a tile that is both doubles twice).

**The winner's lines** (`ScoreWinner.{h,cpp}`; a limit hand never gets them, see below). Points: `Win` 20; `WinTileFromWall` 2 (a self-draw, a loose tile too; not a discard, not a robbed kong); `OnlyWinningTile` 2 (the 13 tiles before the winning tile wait on exactly that one kind, `WinningTiles(PlayerHand)`: the pair to a single, the middle of a chow, the one end of a chow open at one end; a kind of which the seat holds all four copies is no wait). Doubles, each 1 unless stated, read on the reading of four sets and a pair (every reading of the hand is scored, so these decide the best one too): `OnlyPungs` (four pungs or kongs); `NoPoints` (the points of the lines are the 20 alone); `LittleFourWinds` 2 (three sets of winds and a pair of winds); `LittleThreeDragons` (two sets and the pair of dragons); `TerminalsOrHonoursEverywhere` (every set and the pair holds a terminal or an honour; the chows `1 2 3` and `7 8 9` and the pair count); `FullyConcealed` (no open set); `SeveralKongs` (two or three kongs, open or concealed; four is a limit hand). Not about the shape: `LooseTile` (`result.loose`), `LastTile` (`result.lastLiveTile`), `RobbedKong`, `ObviouslyLast` (`visibleWinningTileCopies >= 3`), `CallingFirstHand` (`callingDeclared` with `callingAtDiscardCount == 0`).

**The limit hands** (`ScoreLimit.{h,cpp}`, `FindLimitHand(readings, input)`, called by `ScoreSeat` for a winner before anything is scored): a winner who has one scores `LimitCap(limit)`, and the breakdown is the single line of the hand (`limitHand` set, `points` and `doubles` 0, `raw` = `score`); no other line is read. The hand is found in any reading, and the first match wins in this order (they score the same, only the name differs). A seat that does not win never has one. The readings, as built: `SevenPairsHonours` / `SevenPairsOneSuit` (no melds, `IsSevenPairs`: seven different pairs, of one suit without honours or all honours; seven pairs of honours is named so and not `HonoursOnly`); `ConcealedPungsOneSuit` (four pungs or kongs, none open, so no chow and no set completed by a winning discard; every tile one suit, no honours); `HonoursOnly` (every tile held, concealed and in the melds, an honour, in a hand of four sets and a pair); `TerminalsOnly` (every tile held a terminal, so a chow meld never; four sets and a pair); `AllGreen` (every tile green: bamboo 2 3 4 6 8 and the green dragon; four pungs or kongs and a pair in some reading, so a chow `2 3 4` of bamboo spoils it unless the same tiles read as pungs); `BigThreeDragons` (a pung or kong of each dragon, a fourth pung or kong and the pair both of one suit, no honours); `BigFourWinds` (a pung or kong of each wind and any pair); `FourKongs` (four kongs, open or concealed, and a pair); `ThirteenOrphans` (no melds, `IsThirteenOrphans`, also on the 13-sided wait); `LastTileOneDot` (`result.lastLiveTile` and the winning tile 1 dot); `LooseTileFiveDots` (`result.loose` and the winning tile 5 dots). The two circumstance hands read nothing of the shape.

Readings the owner may change (each is one place): no points means nothing scores but the 20, so a wall tile, a single wait, a flower, a pung or a scoring pair breaks it (`AddNoPoints`); the obviously last of four needs the other three copies open (counted by the caller, `visibleWinningTileCopies`); a set completed by a winning discard or a robbed kong is open, one completed by a self-draw or the pair is concealed (`BuildReadings`); terminals or honours everywhere counts the pair and a chow with a 1 or a 9 (`HoldsTerminalOrHonour`); the calling hand in the first hand is the declaration with the first discard (the model's reading); the order is points times 2^doubles, then the limit, then rounding up to 10.

## Rules for the code to come

- The rules live in `Game/` as plain C++ that a test drives without a scene; `MahjongGame` owns the model, components read it through `MahjongGame::GetActive()` and check that the game is the active one (`engine/src/Modules/Games/CLAUDE.md`, "Gotchas").
- Components subscribe with `SubscribeInScene`; there is no per-frame hook in `IGame`, per-frame work belongs in a component.
- No `ImGui::` in a game component without `ImGui::GetCurrentContext()`: the HUD and the menus are UI canvases of the engine (`engine/src/Modules/BECore/UI/CLAUDE.md`).
- A control API is an `IControllableGame` adapter next to the game, returned from `AsControllable()`; with it comes `be_add_game_control_tests(BagiMahjong ...)` in `CMakeLists.txt`.
- New or moved sources, and every change to a file with `BE_CLASS` / `BE_REFLECT_FIELD`, need `cmake --preset <p>` again (sources are globbed, reflection is generated at configure time).

`tests/FixedWall.h` (header only) lays out a wall tile by tile for the tests of the hand: `FixedWallSpec { hands[4] (13 tiles per seat in deal order), live, loose, liveAfterDeal }`, `MakeFixedWall(spec, flowers, east = 0)` and `MakeModel(spec, east = 0, roundWind, flowers)` (a started `HandModel` on that wall); whatever the spec leaves out is padded from the set (never more than four of a kind; a spec that asks for more fails the test). Use it for every test that needs to know what a seat holds or draws.

`tests/HandTestSupport.h` (header only, namespace `BECore::Mahjong::Tests`) holds what the `HandModel` tests share: the seat names, `T("5p")`, `Error(status)`, `Has`, one `Snapshot(model)` of everything an action could change, `Lap`, the checks of `LegalActions` against `Apply`, and the random-play chooser. A file keeps only what differs (its quiet hands and specs).
