# Mahjong Module Guidelines

## Overview

`MahjongGame` is an `IGame` (name `Mahjong`, scene `MahjongScene`). Executable `BagiMahjong`, module `MahjongModule`, tests `MahjongTests`. Namespace `BECore::Mahjong`. Infrastructure and how a game is registered: `engine/src/Modules/Games/CLAUDE.md`; how this repository was set up around the engine: `engine/NEW_GAME.md` (on the engine's `main`).

**State: every stage of the plan is built: the rules, the match and the computer players, the table session over them (seat setup, driving the computers), the control adapter and its smoke script (`tests/control_smoke.py`, ctest `BagiMahjongControlSmoke`), and the scene: the tile art, the table layout, the table drawn and played by mouse (action buttons, the computers' pace, the hot-seat cover), the HUD, the hints (the waiting row inside the wall ring, the marks on the hand, the `Hint` advisor and its switch in `Setup`), the seat setup (who plays which seat, 16 or 8 hands, new game), the score table at the end of a hand and the standings at the end of the match. What is left is the owner's: a look at a full hand in a real window (the character mark, the touch size, a pause, and the look of the last-move marks, the blink and the move line are the owner's calls; they are in the todo store with the entry). The scoring readings are decided (see "Scoring readings decided by the owner"). The controls that take input are in `Screen` canvases with a floor on their scale (`minScale`), so they stay touch targets in any window; in a narrow window `Actions` and `HintsButton` are swapped for `ActionsCompact` (see "The scene"). The game speaks English and Russian: every word is in the string table (see "Words") and the language is chosen in the `Setup` panel.** The game is registered, starts in Game mode (`mode="Game"` in `config/ApplicationConfig.xml`; set `mode="Editor"` to open it in the scene editor) and shows its scene: the table (`MahjongTableComponent`, see "The scene"). `Game/` holds the rules of classic Chinese mahjong for four seats as plain C++: the tile set, the seeded RNG, the seats, the wall, the deal, the hand shape, `HandModel` (one hand: turns, claims, the three kongs, robbing, winning, the drawn hand, the list of legal actions), scoring and payments (`Score.h`: a seat's points, the doubles for everyone, the winner's lines and the limit hands; `Payments.h`, `ScoreHand.h`: the settlement of a finished hand, see "Scoring") and the match (`Match.h`: 2000 points each, 16 or 8 hands, the deal and the winds, see "Match"). `MahjongGame` owns the match and a `TableSession` over it (`GetSession()`, see "Table session"): `Start()` and `Reset()` begin a new match with a random seed, `StartMatch(seed)` replays one (with `GetSettings()` and the seat setup `GetSeats()`, kept across `Reset()`), and the scene glue (`MahjongTableComponent`) reaches it through `MahjongGame::GetActive()`. `Game/` also holds the computer players (`ComputerPlayer`, `ComputerTable`, see "Computer player"): four of them play whole matches in the tests, and they beat random play clearly (`ComputerStrengthTest`). `TableSession` drives them for the seats that are computers. `MahjongGame::AsControllable()` is the control API (`MahjongGameControl`, see "Control API"): a client plays the person seats over JSON. `CMakeLists.txt` registers the smoke test of the control API (`be_add_game_control_tests`, see "Tests"). The tile art is built (our own, `tools/make_tiles.py`, see "Tile art"); the table of the match is on the scene and can be played by mouse, with the computers answering at a pace and a cover for a hot seat (see "The scene"); the HUD and the seat choice are there too, and so are the score table of a finished hand (`Next hand` settles it) and the standings of a finished match (`New game`), see "The scene"; the rules screen (13 pages in both languages, opened by the `Rules` button of the HUD) is built from `Table/RulesText.h`.

## Wall conventions

The break, the loose tiles and the dead wall are assumptions of this repository (`Wall.h` has the details; the numbers are named constants). The ring is four sides of 17 stacks of two tiles (18 stacks with flowers). East rolls 2d6 `a`: side `(a - 1) % 4` counted from East's own wall is broken; the owner of that side rolls `b`; the break is after `a + b` stacks from the right end of that side. The sides lie clockwise in the ring, so a count that runs past the left end of a side goes on into the wall of the clockwise neighbour, as on a real table, and `GetBreak().index` is the physical position of the break. Drawing starts at the break. The first 2 tiles are the loose reserve (a loose draw is refilled from the live tail), the last 14 are the dead wall and are never drawn.

## HandModel

`Game/HandModel.h` plays one hand; the wall is built outside (`Begin(settings, wall, seatWinds, roundWind)` deals and gives East the first tile). The phase (`Phase`) is `Discard` (the current seat holds one tile too many and acts), `Claim` (a discard is pending and the other seats decide), `RobKong` (an upgraded kong is pending, the other seats may only claim mahjong) or `Finished`. A draw is automatic: every transition that ends in a draw performs it (`Receive`), so a seat only sees a phase it can act in. A drawn bonus tile is laid open and replaced from the loose reserve, again and again; no tile to draw (an empty live wall when a draw is due, an empty reserve when a replacement is due) ends the hand as `ResultKind::Drawn`. Actions (`Discard(seat, tile, declareCalling)`, `DeclareWin(seat)`) return `std::expected<void, HandError>`; a refused action changes nothing. Nothing is compulsory: a seat that could win discards instead if it wants. The calling hand is declared with a discard (`declareCalling`): the 13 tiles that stay must be waiting (`WinningTiles(PlayerHand)`, the copies in the seat's melds counted); `callingAtDiscardCount` is the number of earlier discards of the seat, so "declared in the first hand" is `== 0`; the declaration is not binding afterwards. A self-draw win (`DeclareWin`) needs the declaration and a winning shape and ends the hand with `HandResult { Win, winner, winningTile, WinSource::SelfDraw, from, loose, lastLiveTile }`: `loose` when the tile came from the loose reserve, `lastLiveTile` only for a self-drawn win on the last tile drawn from the live wall (not for a win on a discard, on a loose tile even when its refill emptied the live wall, or by robbing a kong). The shared pile is `Discards()`. Claims: a discard opens `Phase::Claim` only when some other seat has a legal claim (`LegalClaims(seat)`: `ClaimOption { ClaimKind Chow | Pung | Kong | Mahjong, chowFirst }`); otherwise the next seat draws at once. A seat in the window (`PendingClaimSeats()`, `GetClaimStatus`) decides once: `Claim(seat, kind, chowFirst)` or `Pass(seat)` (always allowed; a second decision is `AlreadyDecided`, a seat outside the window `NotYourTurn`, a claim it cannot make `IllegalClaim`). A chow only for the seat after the discarder (`chowFirst` is the lowest tile of the chosen sequence, a seat may have several; never honours, never wrapping); a pung needs two equal tiles, a kong three; mahjong needs the calling-hand declaration (`NotCalling`) and a winning shape with the tile (`CannotWin`). When every seat in the window has decided the best claim wins: mahjong, then pung or kong, then chow; two mahjong claims go to the seat farther counter-clockwise from the discarder (`Offset` larger). The claimed tile leaves the pile and goes into an open `Meld` (`fromDiscard`, `from`, `claimed`); the claimer then discards without drawing (`DeclareWin` is refused: no drawn tile), after a kong it first draws a loose tile, and the turn after its discard goes to the seat after the claimer (seats are skipped). All pass: the seat after the discarder draws. A mahjong claim ends the hand with `WinSource::Discard`, `from` the discarder, the tile joins the winner's concealed tiles. In `Claim` `CurrentSeat()` is the discarder and the pending tile is `Discards().back()`. Kongs of the own turn: `DeclareConcealedKong(seat, tile)` turns four equal concealed tiles into a concealed `Meld` (four tiles left in the hand are no kong; the declaration is never compulsory), `UpgradeKong(seat, tile)` adds the fourth tile to an open pung (the meld turns into a `Kong`) but only with the tile drawn from the wall this turn (a regular or a loose draw: not a copy kept from earlier turns, not right after a claim); both are refused with `IllegalKong` when the hand does not allow them, and both draw a loose tile (`Receive(seat, DrawLoose(), true)`: a bonus tile is replaced again, an empty reserve ends the hand as drawn) before the seat discards. An upgraded kong can be robbed, only to win: when another seat has declared a calling hand that the tile completes, `Phase::RobKong` opens (`CurrentSeat()` is the kong's owner, `PendingTile()` the tile), those seats `Claim(seat, ClaimKind::Mahjong)` or `Pass`, the farther one counter-clockwise from the owner wins, the hand ends with `WinSource::RobbedKong` and `from` the owner, the tile is taken from the kong (the owner keeps a pung); if nobody robs, the kong is completed. A win on a tile from the reserve has `loose` (after a kong or a bonus replacement) and never `lastLiveTile`. `AfterDiscard` decides what follows a discard and `Receive` gives a seat a tile. `PendingTile()` is valid only while a window is open (it is reset when the window closes). `DrawnTile(seat)` is the tile the seat has just drawn (a regular or a loose draw, a bonus tile already replaced): invalid unless the phase is `Discard` and `seat` is `CurrentSeat()` and it really drew (after a claimed pung or chow it is invalid; after a claimed or concealed kong it is the loose tile); a front end calls it only for the seat whose hand it shows face up. `Moves()` is the append-only log of the hand, cleared by `Begin`: `HandMove { MoveKind Discard | ClaimChow | ClaimPung | ClaimKong | ConcealedKong | UpgradeKong, seat, tile, from, meld, declaredCalling }`, one entry per accepted move (a discard, with `meld` `kNoMeld` (-1); a claim once it is carried out, with `from` the discarder and `meld` the index of the new meld; a concealed kong with `tile` left invalid, because it is not public; an upgraded kong, also one that is then robbed, with `meld` the index of the pung); passes, wins, bonus tiles and the end are not in it (`Result()` has the end).

Actions as data (the surface of the computer player and the control API): `HandAction { ActionKind Discard | DeclareWin | ConcealedKong | UpgradeKong | Claim | Pass, tile, declareCalling, claim, chowFirst }` with the factories `MakeDiscard`, `MakeDeclareWin`, `MakeConcealedKong`, `MakeUpgradeKong`, `MakeClaim`, `MakePass`. `LegalActions(seat)` lists what the seat can do now: on its turn a `Discard` of each distinct concealed tile (and the same with `declareCalling` where the rest is waiting), `DeclareWin`, a `ConcealedKong` for each tile held four times, an `UpgradeKong` for the open pung of the tile drawn this turn; in `Claim` / `RobKong` the `LegalClaims` plus `Pass` for a seat that has not decided; empty for every other seat, after the end, and for a number that is no seat. `Apply(seat, action)` dispatches to the method of the kind; an action that is not in the list is refused with that method's error and changes nothing. `Claim` asks first whether the seat is in the window (`NotYourTurn`), only then about the declaration (`NotCalling` / `CannotWin`). `Begin` returns `BadSeatWinds` (and changes nothing) unless the winds hold East exactly once; a model that was never begun is a finished, drawn hand, so a result exists exactly when the phase is `Finished`. `GetHand` and `SeatWind` take a real seat (`IsSeat`); `GetClaimStatus` answers `NotInWindow` for any other number.

## Scoring

`Game/Score.h`: scoring is a pure function, `ScoreSeat(const ScoreInput&)`, so a test builds a hand from notation without a `HandModel`. `ScoreInput { hand, seatWind, roundWind, limit, won, result, visibleWinningTileCopies }` (for the winner the concealed tiles include the winning tile; `result` is read only when `won`). The answer is a `ScoreBreakdown { won, lines, points, doubles, raw, limitHand, score }`: `lines` are `ScoreLine { ScoreKind kind, tile, points, doubles }` of the best reading in a stable order (points: sets, pair, bonus tiles; then doubles); `points` and `doubles` are their sums, `raw` is `points << doubles` (64-bit inside, saturating at `INT_MAX`), `score` is `RoundUpToTen(min(raw, LimitCap(limit)))`, where `LimitCap` is the limit rounded down to a multiple of 10 (0 for a negative one), so a score is a multiple of 10 and never above the limit, also for a limit that is not a multiple of 10 or is `INT_MAX`. The lines run: the winner's points (`Win`, `WinTileFromWall`, `OnlyWinningTile`), the sets, the pair, the bonus tiles, the doubles for everyone, the doubles for the winner. Every number of the rules is a named constant in `Score.h`.

**Readings** (`ScoreReading.h`): a hand is read as sets and a pair before it is scored, and the reading with the largest `raw` wins (a tie keeps the first); all lines of a breakdown come from one reading. The winner: the melds are sets as they are, the concealed tiles go through `Decompose` and each decomposition is a candidate; when the win came from a discard or a robbed kong the set that holds the winning tile is a claimed (open) set, so each place the tile may sit is a candidate of its own (a self-draw: one per decomposition; the winning tile as the pair opens nothing). Four equal concealed tiles never declared a kong read as a concealed pung. A winner whose hand has no four-sets-and-a-pair reading and is no limit hand (seven pairs and thirteen orphans are limit hands, below; or a test input that is no win at all) is read like a seat that does not win (it gets the winner's points and the circumstance doubles). A seat that does not win: the melds as they are, every kind held three or four times in the concealed tiles one concealed pung, and of the kinds held exactly twice the best scoring pair; chows in the concealed tiles are not looked for.

**Lines so far:** a pung or kong scores 2 (simples, open) times 2 for terminals, winds and dragons, times 2 when concealed, times 4 for a kong; a pair of dragons, of the own wind and of the round wind 2 each (own and round at once 4); a bonus tile 4; a chow 0. Doubles for everyone: a pung or kong of dragons, of the own wind, of the round wind 1 each (`DragonSet`, `OwnWindSet`, `RoundWindSet`); one suit with honours 1, without honours 3 (read on all tiles of the seat, the melds' too; bonus tiles are not looked at); a flower or season whose number is the own wind's or the round wind's 1 each (`OwnBonus`, `RoundBonus`; a tile that is both doubles twice).

**The winner's lines** (`ScoreWinner.{h,cpp}`; a limit hand never gets them, see below). Points: `Win` 20; `WinTileFromWall` 2 (a self-draw, a loose tile too; not a discard, not a robbed kong); `OnlyWinningTile` 2 (the 13 tiles before the winning tile wait on exactly that one kind, `WinningTiles(PlayerHand)`: the pair to a single, the middle of a chow, the one end of a chow open at one end; a kind of which the seat holds all four copies is no wait). Doubles, each 1 unless stated, read on the reading of four sets and a pair (every reading of the hand is scored, so these decide the best one too): `OnlyPungs` (four pungs or kongs); `NoPoints` (the points of the lines are the 20 alone); `LittleFourWinds` 2 (three sets of winds and a pair of winds); `LittleThreeDragons` (two sets and the pair of dragons); `TerminalsOrHonoursEverywhere` (every set and the pair holds a terminal or an honour; the chows `1 2 3` and `7 8 9` and the pair count); `FullyConcealed` (no open set); `SeveralKongs` (two or three kongs, open or concealed; four is a limit hand). Not about the shape: `LooseTile` (`result.loose`), `LastTile` (`result.lastLiveTile`), `RobbedKong`, `ObviouslyLast` (`visibleWinningTileCopies >= 3`), `CallingFirstHand` (`callingDeclared` with `callingAtDiscardCount == 0`).

**The limit hands** (`ScoreLimit.{h,cpp}`, `FindLimitHand(readings, input)`, called by `ScoreSeat` for a winner before anything is scored): a winner who has one scores `LimitCap(limit)`, and the breakdown is the single line of the hand (`limitHand` set, `points` and `doubles` 0, `raw` = `score`); no other line is read. The hand is found in any reading, and the first match wins in this order (they score the same, only the name differs). A seat that does not win never has one. The readings, as built: `SevenPairsHonours` / `SevenPairsOneSuit` (no melds, `IsSevenPairs`: seven different pairs, of one suit without honours or all honours; seven pairs of honours is named so and not `HonoursOnly`); `ConcealedPungsOneSuit` (four pungs or kongs, none open, so no chow and no set completed by a winning discard; every tile one suit, no honours); `HonoursOnly` (every tile held, concealed and in the melds, an honour, in a hand of four sets and a pair); `TerminalsOnly` (every tile held a terminal, so a chow meld never; four sets and a pair); `AllGreen` (every tile green: bamboo 2 3 4 6 8 and the green dragon; four pungs or kongs and a pair in some reading, so a chow `2 3 4` of bamboo spoils it unless the same tiles read as pungs); `BigThreeDragons` (a pung or kong of each dragon, a fourth pung or kong and the pair both of one suit, no honours); `BigFourWinds` (a pung or kong of each wind and any pair); `FourKongs` (four kongs, open or concealed, and a pair); `ThirteenOrphans` (no melds, `IsThirteenOrphans`, also on the 13-sided wait); `LastTileOneDot` (`result.lastLiveTile` and the winning tile 1 dot); `LooseTileFiveDots` (`result.loose` and the winning tile 5 dots). The two circumstance hands read the circumstances and need a complete reading (a winning shape) besides.

**Payments and the bridge** (`Payments.h`, `ScoreHand.h`). `Settle(rounded, winner, east)` returns the change of the four seats (`SeatScores`, zero-sum): every loser pays the winner the winner's score (a loser that scored more still pays), the losers settle pairwise (the lower score pays the higher the difference, equal: nothing), and every payment between two seats of which one is East is times `kEastPaymentFactor` (2), so East pays and receives double; `kNoSeat` (a drawn hand) gives zeros. `SettleHand(const HandModel&)` takes a finished hand and returns a `HandSettlement { kind, winner, east, roundWind, breakdowns[4], scores[4], deltas[4] }`: each seat is scored by `ScoreSeat` from its own hand, the winds and the limit of the model (the winner with `Result()`), also in a drawn hand (the breakdowns are for the score table), and `deltas` is `Settle` of the rounded `scores`. `visibleWinningTileCopies` is `CountVisibleCopies(model, tile)`: the copies of the winning kind in `Discards()` plus in the open melds (`!concealed`) of all four seats, the winner's too; the winning tile itself is not counted (a discard win took it out of the pile, a robbed kong out of the kong, so the owner's pung counts three); concealed kongs and concealed tiles are not visible.

## Match

`Game/Match.h`: `Match` plays a game over `HandModel`s. `Start(settings, seed)` (`std::expected<void, MatchError>`: `BadSettings` unless `IsPlayable(settings)`, i.e. `handsPerMatch` is a positive multiple of 4, and then nothing changes) seeds the match's own `MahjongRng`; the draw for East (`RollSeatWinds`) comes first, then one `MakeWall` per hand, so one seed is one match. Each seat starts with `settings.startingScore` (2000), the first hand is dealt at once and `Hand()` is played through its actions. When the hand is `Finished`, `EndHand()` settles it (`SettleHand`), adds the `deltas` to `Scores()`, keeps the `HandSettlement` in `History()`, moves the deal and the count, and deals the next hand or finishes the match (`MatchPhase::Finished`: the last hand stays in `Hand()`, `EndHand` is refused with `NotInHand`); before the hand is finished it is `HandNotFinished`. `ApplySettlement(settlement)` is the same step for a settlement made elsewhere (the tests of the rules feed it made-up ones; it does not look at `Hand()`). `Standings()` lists the seats best first, equal points in seat order. The bookkeeping is pure: `NextProgress(MatchProgress{handsPlayed, east}, kind, winner, settings)`, `RoundWindOf(handsPlayed, settings)`, `IsMatchOver`.

The rules, as built: the first East is the seat that holds East after `RollSeatWinds`; the winds of a hand are `MakeSeatWinds(east)`. A hand won by East: East deals again; won by another seat: the deal moves to `NextSeat(east)` (that seat becomes East, the winds move on). **Every hand played counts toward `handsPerMatch`, an East repeat too** (`kEastRepeatCountsAsHand = true` in `Match.h` is the one place; with `false` an East repeat does not count and a round ends after `handsPerMatch / 4` deals, which makes the number of hands depend on the play and unbounded in theory). The round wind is `Wind(handsPlayed / (handsPerMatch / 4))`: East for hands 0..3, South for 4..7, ... North for 12..15 (0..1, 2..3, ... in the short game of 8); the match is over when `handsPlayed == handsPerMatch`. A drawn hand: no payments; with `replayDrawnHand` (the default) the same East deals again and the hand does not count, otherwise it counts and the deal moves on (East did not win). The settings `handsPerMatch`, `replayDrawnHand`, `limit` and `startingScore` are in `MahjongSettings.h`. `MahjongGame` plays with `GetSettings()`: the defaults (`MahjongSettings{}`) until `SetSettings`, kept across `Reset()`. Once the match is over `East()`, `Winds()` and `RoundWind()` describe the hand that would come next.

## Computer player

`Game/ComputerView.h`, `Game/ComputerTurn.h`, `Game/ComputerClaim.h`, `Game/ComputerPlayer.h`. A computer player never gets the `HandModel`: it gets a `TableView` made by `MakeTableView(model, seat)`, which holds the seat's own hand and what lies open (the open melds of the others, the count of their concealed kongs, bonus tiles and discards, the declarations, the winds, the live-wall count, the pile as `visible` copies per kind, `legal` = `LegalActions(seat)`) and nothing else, so no peeking is true by construction and the control API can reuse the same view. `UnseenCopies(view, tile)` is `4 - visible` (the pile, the own tiles and every open meld are visible; a concealed kong of another seat is not; in `Phase::RobKong` the tile of the kong is visible to everyone). The player chooses only elements of `view.legal`, with its own `MahjongRng` used only to break ties.

The turn (`ChooseTurnAction`, `Phase::Discard`), the first step that applies wins: (1) `DeclareWin` when listed; (2) the calling hand: of the discards that declare it, the one that leaves the most unseen winning copies (`WinningTiles` of the 13 that stay, seven pairs and orphans included; ties by `KeepValue`, then the rng), a declaration is never worse because a win needs it; (3) a kong (`ConcealedKong`, `UpgradeKong`) when the hand without it is no worse: the best discard of the hand as it is against the hand without the four tiles and one more meld (an upgrade: without the fourth tile), the shanten not higher and, for a waiting hand, the `AcceptanceOf` not lower; (4) after the declaration, the discard that keeps the hand waiting with the most unseen winning copies (normally the drawn tile); (5) otherwise `BestDiscard`: the discards that leave the lowest `Shanten`, among them the highest `AcceptanceOf` (the unseen copies of the `ImprovingTiles`), then the lowest `KeepValue` (a tile no use to the seat 0, a terminal 1, a simple 2, a dragon / the own wind / the round wind 3), then the rng. Seven pairs and thirteen orphans are not aimed at (`Shanten` looks at four sets and a pair only) but are declared and won when the model lists them; flushes and the other limit hands are not aimed at either (the player has one strength level and no personality).

The claims (`ChooseClaimAction`, `Phase::Claim` and `Phase::RobKong`), the first step that applies wins: (1) a `Mahjong` claim whenever listed (the only claim of a robbed kong); (2) `Pass` when the hand as it is (13 tiles) waits, `Shanten` 0 (it stays concealed and takes mahjong only, also against a pung of dragons); (3) each listed pung, kong and chow is rated by `RateClaim`: the hand after the claim (`ConcealedAfterClaim`: the tiles of the set that the seat holds are out, one more meld; the pending tile was never in the hand), for a pung and a chow the `BestDiscardRating` of the 11 tiles, for a kong (10 tiles, the loose tile is unknown) the shanten and acceptance of the hand as it stands, so both compare with the shanten of the hand before. A claim is acceptable when its shanten is lower than the hand's, or equal for a pung or kong of a value tile (`IsValueTile`: a dragon, the own wind, the round wind, a double); of the acceptable ones the lowest shanten, then the highest acceptance, then `ClaimRank` (a kong over a pung over a chow), then the rng; (4) no acceptable claim: `Pass`. No defence: every loser pays the winner the same, so a discard that wins costs the discarder nothing extra. `ComputerPlayer` (`Choose(view)` dispatches on `view.phase` to the turn or the claim rule; `Decide(model, seat)` makes the view first and is empty for a seat with nothing to do) has its own `MahjongRng` for ties. `ComputerTable` is four players, seat `i` seeded `SeatSeed(tableSeed, i)` (one roll of an `MahjongRng` seeded `tableSeed * 4 + i`, a fixed mixing, never a draw from the machine): `NextActor(model)` is `CurrentSeat()` in `Phase::Discard` and the first of `PendingClaimSeats()` in a window, `Step(model)` applies one action of the next actor through `Apply` and returns it (`StepResult`; it never falls back to another action, so a refusal is a defect and comes back as the error, `WrongPhase` on a finished hand), `PlayHand` steps to the end and `PlayMatch(Match&)` also calls `EndHand` until the match is finished. The table does not know a seat setup (which seats are people): the setup and the driving of the computers live in `TableSession` (see "Table session"), which `MahjongGame` owns.

## Table session

`Game/TableSession.h`: the one object every front end (control API, table component, HUD) drives, so that "who acts next" and "when does the state change" exist once. `TableSession(Match&)` keeps a reference to the match (owned by `MahjongGame`, declared before the session). `StartMatch(settings, seed, seats)` is `Match::Start` plus a `ComputerTable` of the same seed and the `SeatSetup` (`SeatKind Person | Computer` per seat; `kDefaultSeatSetup`: seat 0 a person, the others computers); `BadSettings` changes nothing. `GetPhase()` is `SessionPhase`: `Playing` (some seat acts), `HandOver` (the hand is `Finished`, waiting for `NextHand()`), `MatchOver` (the last hand is settled, or no match was started). `Actor()` is `ComputerTable::NextActor` while `Playing` (`CurrentSeat()` in a turn, the first pending seat in a claim window or a robbing window; the outcome does not depend on the order, so a person decides, then the next pending seat is asked), `kNoSeat` otherwise. `StepComputer()` plays one action of a computer actor (`WrongPhase` unless `Playing`, `NotYourTurn` for a person); `RunComputers()` steps until a person has to act or the hand is over and returns the steps (cap `kMaxComputerSteps`, a defect asserts); `PerformPerson(action)` applies `Apply` for a person actor (`NotYourTurn` for a computer, the error of the model otherwise); `NextHand()` is `Match::EndHand` (the settlement of a `HandOver` hand before it is applied is `SettleHand(Hand())`, after it `LastSettlement()`). `Version()` grows by one on every change a front end must see (a start, a step, an accepted action, a settled hand, a `Reveal` that changes the viewer), never goes back, and a refused call leaves it as it was.

Hot seat: with exactly one person that seat is the `Viewer()` always (no cover); with none the viewer is `kNoSeat` (a spectator sees no concealed hand until the hand is over); with two or more the viewer is the person who acted last (an accepted `PerformPerson` makes the actor the viewer) and becomes `kNoSeat` as soon as another person has to act (after a start, a step, an action or a settled hand). `HandoffPending()` is true while `Playing` with two or more people, a person to act, and that person not the viewer; `Reveal()` makes the actor the viewer. The same person acting again (computers in between do not matter) keeps it clear. In `HandOver` and `MatchOver` all hands are shown and front ends read `GetPhase()`, not the viewer. A front end that paces the computers calls `StepComputer()` once per period; the control API calls `RunComputers()`. A match is started only through the session (`MahjongGame::StartMatch`, `Reset`): one started through the non-const `GetMatch()` has no computer players, and `StepComputer` answers `WrongPhase` for it.

## Match log

`Game/MatchLog.h`: every match can be written down as a list of `MatchLogEvent`s, plain data with a `kind` (`Start`: seed, settings, who plays which seat; `Hand`: a hand was dealt (number, East, round wind, winds, the dealt `concealed` and `bonus` tiles of all four seats, East's 14 included: nothing is hidden, the log is the owner's); `Action`: seat, `by` (`SeatKind::Person` | `Computer`) and the `HandAction`; `Settlement`: the `HandSettlement` of the hand; `End`: final scores and standings). `IMatchLogSink::OnEvent` receives them; `MatchLog` is a sink that keeps them (`Events()`, `Clear()`), `operator==` of an event compares the members of its kind (a settlement on kind, winner, east, round wind, scores and deltas).

**The hook is `TableSession`** (`SetLogSink(IMatchLogSink*)`, not owned, null by default, changeable at any time), because the table component, the HUD and the control API all drive the session, so one hook logs every front end. Order: `StartMatch` emits `Start`, `Hand`; each accepted `StepComputer` / `PerformPerson` emits `Action` (the actor's seat, `by` from `KindOf`); `NextHand` emits `Settlement`, then the `Hand` of the next deal, or `End` after the last hand. **Not logged:** a refused call, `Reveal`, a start with bad settings (a pass is an action and is logged), and a match started behind the session's back through the non-const `GetMatch()` (the session never sees it).

`ReplayMatchLog(events, match)` plays a log on a fresh `Match`: `Match::Start(settings, seed)`, `Hand().Apply(seat, action)` for each `Action`, `EndHand()` for each `Settlement`; no computer players, the actions are in the log. The `Hand`, `Settlement` and `End` events are checked against what the match makes (the deal, kind / winner / east / round wind / scores / deltas, the final scores and standings), and the result is nothing or a `ReplayError { index, problem }` naming the first event that disagrees.

**The JSON form** (`src/Mahjong/MatchLogJson.{h,cpp}`, outside `Game/`, which stays free of JSON): one object per line, key `event`, tiles in the notation of `Tile.h`, winds by name, seats as numbers 0..3. `start`: `version` (`kMatchLogFormatVersion` 1), `seed`, `settings` {`handsPerMatch`, `flowers`, `replayDrawnHand`, `limit`, `startingScore`}, `seats` ("PCCC" as in the control `reset`); `hand`: `number`, `east`, `roundWind`, `winds` (4), `deal` (4 x {`concealed`, `bonus`}, East's 14 included); `action`: `seat`, `by` (`person` | `computer`), `action` (exactly `ActionToJson`, the control API's action object); `settlement`: `kind` (`win` | `drawn`), `winner` (seat | null), `east`, `roundWind`, `scores`, `deltas`, `seats` (4 x {`breakdown`: `BreakdownToJson`}; the winner's lines are in the winner's breakdown); `end`: `scores`, `standings` (seats, best first). `EventToJson(event)` / `EventFromJson(json)` convert, `ParseMatchLog(text)` reads a whole file (blank lines skipped) into the vector `ReplayMatchLog` takes. Reading is checked with the helpers of `BECore/Control/Json.h`: a missing or wrong-typed member, an unknown `event`, a bad tile, seat letter or wind name is an error text naming the member (and, from `ParseMatchLog`, `line N:`); a settlement is read back from `kind`, `winner`, `east`, `roundWind`, `scores` and `deltas` only, its `seats` (the breakdowns) are for the reader of the file.

**The file** (`src/Mahjong/MatchLogWriter.{h,cpp}`, owned by `MahjongGame` and set as the sink of its session, so every start (`Start`, `Reset`, `StartMatch`, a control `reset`, the HUD) writes a log): one file per match, `logs/matches/YYYY-MM-DDTHH-MM-SS_seed<seed>.jsonl` (local time; a name that exists gets `-2`, `-3` ... before the extension, because the same seed can start twice in a second), one `MatchLogJson` line per event, flushed per line so a crash or a match given up keeps the log up to its last event. The file is opened at the `Start` event (one info line of the engine log, `MatchLogWriter: the match is logged to <path>`), closed at `End`, at the next `Start` and by the destructor. The folder is `MatchLogDirectory()` (default `logs/matches`, relative to the working directory, beside `engine.log`; an empty path switches logging off), changed with `SetMatchLogDirectory(path)`; `logs/` is in `.gitignore`. The writer never fails the game: a folder or file that cannot be made, or a failed write, is one warning and the match goes on unlogged. `MahjongGame::GetMatchLog()` is the writer (`Path()` the file of the current match, `IsOpen()`). Where the files land: a run of the game or of `BagiMahjong --headless --control` writes under the working directory it was started from; the gtests point the folder at a temporary one for the whole process (the global environment of `MatchLogFileTest.cpp`, removed at the end), so none leaves `logs/` behind; the game that `BagiMahjongControlSmoke` starts has the build folder as its working directory (the engine's `smoke.py` runs it there), so its logs land in `build/ClangmacOS/logs/matches/`: git-ignored (`/build/`), a few kilobytes each, and not cleaned up. A file is opened with `std::ios::noreplace`, so an existing log is never overwritten (the `-N` suffix is tried up to `kMaxCopySuffix`, then the match goes on unlogged).

## Control API

`MahjongGameControl.{h,cpp}` (next to `MahjongGame`, owned by it, returned from `AsControllable()`): `get_state`, `list_actions`, `perform_action`, `reset` of the engine's game control (`engine/src/Modules/Games/CLAUDE.md`, "Control API") over a `MahjongGame&`. A client plays the **person seats** of the session; after every action the adapter calls `RunComputers()`, so the computers have answered when the call returns (the pace of a screen is the table component's business). With no person (`"seats": "CCCC"`) `reset` and `next_hand` play the hand to its end, so a client watches a match through `get_state` and `next_hand`. `set_state` is unsupported; `no_active_model` before the first match. `GetStateVersion()` is `TableSession::Version()`.

State (`get_state`) is the view of the person who has to act (`Actor()`), else of the first person seat, built with `MakeTableView`, so a client never sees what a computer player could not. With no person seat at all the client is a spectator: `seat`, `seatWind` are null and `hand` is `[]` (the hands are in `result` once the hand is over, and `yourTurn` is false). `phase` (`playing` | `hand_over` | `match_over`), `seat`, `seatWind`, `roundWind`, `east`, `hand` (the concealed tiles of `seat`, sorted), `turn` (`phase` `discard` | `claim` | `rob_kong` | `finished`, `current`, `pendingTile`, `yourTurn`), `seats` (4 x `kind`, `wind`, `score`, `concealedCount` (the number of concealed tiles, public), `melds`, `concealedKongs`, `bonus`, `discardCount`, `calling`), `discards` (`tile`, `seat`, in order), `wall` (live tiles left), `handsPlayed`, `handsTotal`, `version`, and in `hand_over` / `match_over` `result`. Tiles are the notation of `Tile.h` (`5p`, `Ew`, `Rd`, `F1`). A meld has `kind` (`chow` | `pung` | `kong`), `tiles`, `concealed`, `from`, `fromDiscard`, `claimed`; every seat lists its open melds, the seat of the view also its concealed kongs. `yourTurn` is the thing a client reads (the seat has actions to choose from); `turn.phase` only says what the hand waits for. `result` (all four hands are public once the hand is over): `kind` (`win` | `drawn`), `winner`, `winningTile`, `source` (`discard` | `self_draw` | `robbed_kong`), `from`, `loose`, `lastLiveTile`, `seats` (4 x `concealed`, `melds`, `bonus`, `breakdown` {`won`, `lines` [{`kind` as in `ScoreKind`, `tile`, `points`, `doubles`}], `points`, `doubles`, `raw`, `score`, `limitHand`}), `scores`, `deltas`. In `hand_over` the scores of `seats` are those before the settlement.

Actions (`list_actions`, each accepted by `perform_action` as it is; `ActionToJson` / `ActionFromJson`): `{"type":"discard","tile","declareCalling"}`, `win`, `{"type":"concealed_kong","tile"}`, `upgrade_kong`, `{"type":"claim","claim":"chow","first"}` (`first` is the lowest tile of the sequence) / `"pung"` / `"kong"` / `"mahjong"`, `pass`, and `next_hand` (only in `hand_over`; the outcome has `settlement` {`kind`, `winner`, `scores`, `deltas`}). The list is `LegalActions` of the person who has to act; empty when a computer acts (a screen paces them) and in `match_over`. The outcome of `perform_action` is `{"applied", "phase", "version"}`. `illegal_action` is a well-formed action that is not listed now (or `next_hand` outside `hand_over`); `invalid_params` is a malformed one (no or unknown `type`, a missing tile or `first`, a tile that is not a string or not in the notation, `declareCalling` not a boolean); fields a kind ignores are ignored. All client data goes through the checked helpers of `BECore/Control/Json.h`.

`reset` takes `{"seed": 0..4294967295, "seats": "PCCC" (four letters P or C), "hands": 8 | 16, "flowers": bool}`, all optional; what is left out keeps the setting of the game (the seed: the one of the last `reset`, or 1), and the seats and settings that were given stay for the next match (`SetSeats` / `SetSettings`). Anything else is `invalid_params` and nothing changes.

## Scoring readings decided by the owner

The owner went through these readings on 2026-11-08 and kept them as built; each is still a change in one place.

- No points: nothing scores but the 20, so a wall tile, a single wait, a flower, a pung or a scoring pair breaks it (`AddNoPoints`, `ScoreWinner.cpp`).
- Obviously last of four: the other three copies of the winning kind are open, in the discards and the open sets of all seats; a concealed kong or the winner's own tiles do not count (`CountVisibleCopies`, `ScoreHand.cpp`; `kObviouslyLastCopies`).
- Calling hand in the first hand: declared with the first discard, `callingAtDiscardCount == 0` (the model's reading, `HandModel`; the line is `AddHandCircumstances`, `ScoreWinner.cpp`).
- Terminals or honours in every set and the pair: the pair counts and so does a chow with a 1 or a 9 (`HoldsTerminalOrHonour`, `ScoreWinner.cpp`).
- A set completed by a winning discard or a robbed kong is open (it decides fully concealed, concealed-pung points and the limit hand of concealed pungs); by a self-draw it is concealed, the pair opens nothing (`AddDecompositionReadings`, `ScoreReading.cpp`).
- Own and round flower: each matching flower or season doubles, one that is both doubles twice (`AddBonusDoubles`, `Score.cpp`). The flowers and seasons are not offered to the player (the settings keep `flowers = false`, no screen turns them on); the code stays for the control `reset` option `flowers`, used by tests and tools.
- The order: points times 2^doubles, then the limit, then rounding up to 10 (`ScoreSeat`, `Score.cpp`).
- The only winning tile: the 13 tiles before the winning tile wait on that one kind (`WaitsOnlyForTheWinningTile`, `ScoreWinner.cpp`).
- The limit hands: one suit means no honours, all green is pungs and a pair only, the big three dragons' fourth set and pair are of one suit, four kongs open or concealed (`ScoreLimit.cpp`).
- A drawn hand pays nothing; a seat that does not win scores the sets that stand and its best pair (`Settle`, `Payments.cpp`; `ReadStandingHand`, `ScoreReading.cpp`).
- Every hand played counts toward the 16 or 8, an East repeat too (`kEastRepeatCountsAsHand`, `Match.h`).
- The starting score 2000 and the limit are settings (`startingScore`, `limit` in `MahjongSettings.h`).

## Words

The words of the game are in `config/UIStrings.xml` (the engine's `UIStrings`, `CoreManager::GetUIStrings()`, loaded at start-up), English (`en`, the default) and Russian (`ru`), keys `area.name` (`lang.en` / `lang.ru`, each language written in itself; `wind.<Wind>`, `suit.<TileSuit>`, the `tile.*` patterns, `seat.*`, `score.kind.<ScoreKind>` and `score.of` / `.bracket` / `.points` / `.doubles` / `.limit` for the names of seats, tiles and score lines, `handover.*` / `matchover.*` for the sentences of the end screens (`handover.win.selfDraw`, `.drawn`, `.summary.*`, `.seat.won`, `.more`, `matchover.wins` / `.tied` / `.row`, and the two button words `handover.next` / `.finish`); `button.*` / `hint.*` / `cover.*` for the action buttons, their hint and the hot-seat cover; `hints.*` / `setup.hints` / `.on` / `.off` for the hints (`hints.waitCaption`, `hints.adviseButton`, and one sentence `hints.advice.discard` / `.discardCalling` / `.win` / `.concealedKong` / `.upgradeKong` / `.claimMahjong` / `.claimPung` / `.claimKong` / `.claimChow` (`{first}`, `{second}`, `{third}`) / `.pass`, built by `AdviceText` in `Table/Hints.h` (the captions `hints.waitCaption` and `hints.adviseButton` are said by the scene)), `hud.*` (the round, wall and turn texts, the turn words for the end of a hand / match, `New game`), `move.*` (the sentences of the move line: `move.discard`, `.discardCalling`, `.chow`, `.pung`, `.kong`, `.concealedKong`, `.upgradeKong`, with `{seat}` and `{tile}`), `setup.*` (title, `language`, `seat1..4`, `person`, `computer`, `start`) and `matchover.newGame` for the HUD, the seat choice and the standings). Two users: the scene says `text="{#key}"`; code that builds a sentence reads a key with `Tr(strings, key)` / `Tr(strings, key, {{"n", "2"}})` (`Table/Words.h`: the current language, else the default, else the key itself; `{name}` placeholders replaced, an unknown one left as written). The helpers take a `const UIStrings&`, so the pure `Table/` code and its tests need no engine services. The order of words in a sentence belongs to the translation: one key with placeholders, never pieces joined in code. `MahjongGame::GetLanguage()` / `SetLanguage(PoolString)` keep the language for the session (`kDefaultLanguage` "en"; an id the table does not have is refused and nothing changes; kept across `Reset()` and `StartMatch`, applied to the table at every start). The control API (ids, tile notation, JSON) stays English in every language. The turn and dealer marks of the HUD (`>` and `*`) are symbols, not words, and stay in code. The rules pages (`Table/RulesText.h`, keys `rules.<page>.title` / `.line1..`, `rules.pager`, every key written as a literal in `RulesText.cpp`, one block in each language after the `hud.*` keys) never retype a number or a button word: a number is a `{placeholder}` (`{dealt}`, `{winning}`, `{loose}`, `{deadWall}`, ...) that `FormatRulesPage` fills from the named constants of the rules, a button word is `{pung}`, `{kong}`, `{chow}`, `{pass}`, `{mahjong}`, `{declare}` from `WordOf`, and `RulesTextTest` fails on a digit outside a placeholder in any `rules.*` text of either language (a number written as a word, "four sets and a pair", is the shape of the rule and fine). There are 13 pages; the scoring pages (points, doubles, limit hands, payments, the match) take their numbers from `Score.h`, `Payments.h`, `Match.h` and `MahjongSettings.h` (`{winPoints}`, `{limit}`, `{eastFactor}`, `{startScore}`, ...) and their items are `ScoreLineText` lines built with the constants, so a name and its number are those of the score table; `RulesTextTest` holds them to `ScoreSeat` and `Settle` and holds every `ScoreKind` from `DragonSet` on (but the flowers' ones) to a page. New words go into `config/UIStrings.xml` in both languages, never as a literal in code or in the scene (`StringsTest` holds that: parity, every key used, no plain word in a `text`). In tests the fixture `MahjongTableTestBase` loads the shipped file and sets `en`, so the English expectations hold; a test that needs Russian calls `MahjongGame::SetLanguage` itself (a start applies the language of the game over the table's, so one set on the table before a start is lost) and tests of words loop over `GetLanguages()` (`ForEachLanguage`).

## Tile art

The tiles are our own, drawn by `tools/make_tiles.py` (owner decision: no third-party tile set and no font file). The script is a small software rasterizer (rectangles, circles, thick lines, polygons, a 5x7 bitmap font of our own, drawn at 2x and averaged down) and one function per kind of face, so the art is the script: change it and run `python3 tools/make_tiles.py`, which writes `assets/tiles/tiles.png` and `config/TextureLibrary.xml` and prints the cell table. `python3 tools/make_tiles.py --check` exits 0 when the committed files are what the generator makes now (decoded pixels and the XML text, not the PNG bytes, because zlib output differs between versions) and when the cells are sound: all 43 pairwise different, opaque in the middle, transparent corners, the unused cells empty. Run it after every change to the script. It also holds that every face (cells 0..41) has index pixels in `INDEX_BOX` and that the art alone leaves that box untouched ("has no index", "has art under the index"; the back is exempt). `python3 tools/make_tiles.py --preview OUT.png` writes a contact sheet of the 43 cells at four sizes (native 96 x 128, 45 x 60 the viewer's hand, 30 x 40 the viewer's meld, 24 x 32 a discard) on the felt colour, for judging readability; `OUT` must be outside the repository (the picture is never committed), and `--preview` and `--check` exclude each other.

The atlas is 8 columns x 6 rows of cells of 96 x 128 pixels (768 x 768, RGBA, filter 0). The cell of a tile is its id (`Tile.h`: dots 0..8, bamboo 9..17, characters 18..26, winds, dragons, flowers, seasons), cell 42 is the back, the rest is transparent. The sprites of `config/TextureLibrary.xml` are `Tile_<notation>` (`Tile_5p`, `Tile_Ew`, `Tile_Rd`, `Tile_F1`, `Tile_T4`) and `Tile_Back`, with `texturePath="assets/tiles/tiles.png"`; `TextureLibrary` finds the file by its stem and reads its child `sprites`, the document element can have any name. `Table/TileAtlas.h` gives the same cells by arithmetic (`SourceRect(tile)`, `BackRect()`, `SpriteName(tile)`), and `TileSpritesTest` holds the XML, the PNG header and the arithmetic together, so the script's `CELL_W`, `CELL_H`, `COLUMNS` (the one place of the geometry) and `TileAtlas.h` cannot drift. Faces: dots and bamboo as the usual patterns (the one bamboo is a stalk with leaves), characters as a red digit over a black mark that is not a real glyph, winds as a ring with `E S W N`, the dragons as a red emblem with a cross, an empty blue frame and a green diamond, flowers and seasons as a coloured disc with `F1..F4` / `T1..T4` and that many dots (warm colours for flowers, cooler for seasons). **The corner index:** every face but the back has a small index in its top left corner so that a tile reads at the size of a discard (24 x 32, a quarter of the cell, where one font pixel is one screen pixel): a glyph of the 5x7 font at `INDEX_PIXEL` = 4.0 cell units per font pixel at (`INDEX_X`, `INDEX_Y`) = (8, 8), and below it a sign of 16 units. Dots, bamboo and characters: the digit `1..9` in black over a ring (blue, ivory hole) / a short green stick with a red band / a small red mark of our own (not a real glyph); winds the letter `E S W N` in the wind colour; the red and green dragons the letters `R` and `G` in their colour, the White dragon a hollow blue frame (a `W` would be taken for the West wind); flowers and seasons the number in black over the letter `F` / `T` in the colour of the disc (the disc keeps its `F1` text). A light colour gets a dark outline instead of a plate. The index sits on the plain face, so the pattern is drawn under a view of the `Canvas` (scale `ART_SCALE` = 0.72, anchored to the bottom right of the face, lines, rings and glyphs scale together) and the index afterwards in cell coordinates; the character mark of the art is scaled, not redrawn. The back has no index. `INDEX_PIXEL`, `INDEX_X`, `INDEX_Y`, `INDEX_BOX` and `ART_SCALE` are the named constants at the top of the script; a bigger index reads better and shrinks the art.

## The table layout

`Table/TableLayout.h` turns "what is on the table" into rectangles, with no scene and no renderer. `TableInput { viewer, concealedCount[4], melds[4] (kind only matters: 3 tiles, a kong 4), bonusCount[4], discardSeats (the seat of each entry of `Discards()`), wallRemaining }` goes in, `TableLayout { viewer, viewWidth, viewHeight, slots }` comes out; a `TileSlot { rect, role (HandTile | MeldTile | BonusTile | DiscardTile | WallStack), seat, index, part }` says what it is for (`index`: the concealed tile, the meld (and `part` the tile in it), the bonus tile, the entry of the pile, the wall stack). The component maps `index` to tiles; the layout knows no tile.

The design is 1280 x 720 (`kViewWidth` / `kViewHeight`; `config/SceneConfig.xml` repeats them as `viewWidth` / `viewHeight` of `MahjongScene` and `MahjongTableUiTest` compares the two); another view size scales it uniformly to fit and centres it. The viewer's seat is at the bottom; `SeatPosition(viewer, seat)` is 0 bottom, 1 right, 2 top, 3 left (counter-clockwise, as the seats are numbered). The viewer's concealed tiles are one row of large tiles in input order, its melds to the right of them, its bonus tiles a small row above; the opponents' hands are upright small tiles, a row at the top and columns at the sides (melds and bonus tiles in columns / rows beside them). The discards of each seat lie in a block in front of it, 6 per row in the order discarded, five rows without overlap and more rows squeezed together (a zone never grows). The wall is a ring of `ceil(Remaining() / 2)` square stacks (at most 72, a 144-tile set) in a rectangle of cells in the middle (centred between the top and the bottom discard block, so the plates under the three keep one gap), filled clockwise from the top left corner; the physical break is not tracked after the deal, so this is a simplification, not the real wall. A zone holds the most the rules allow (14 concealed tiles, four kongs, 8 bonus tiles); more is squeezed, so no slot leaves the view and no zone overlaps another (the HUD gets the corners and the free parts of the middle). `TableLayout::zones` (`TableZones`) is the ground of the plates: the discard block of each seat (`discards[seat]`, whatever the pile holds) and the wall ring (`wall`); a zone holds its slots and overlaps no other zone and no hand, meld or bonus slot. `TableInput::drawnIndex` (default -1) is the index, in the viewer's concealed tiles, of the tile just drawn: its slot keeps its `index` and its place in `slots`, but its rect lies at the right end of the row after the others with a gap of `kDrawnTileGap` (12), and the others close up in order; the hand strip is `kDrawnTileGap` longer than 14 tiles (a `static_assert` holds the gap inside `kViewerRowGap`, so 14 tiles and the gap end before the melds); an index out of range, and every seat but the viewer, is ignored. `TileSlot::claimEdge` (`Edge`: `None`, `Left`, `Right`, `Top`, `Bottom`) is set on one `MeldTile` slot of an open meld that came from a discard (`fromDiscard`, not concealed): the claimed tile (a chow: `claimed.Rank() - first.Rank()`; a pung or kong: part 0 when it came from the previous seat, 1 across, 2 from the next seat, an upgraded kong keeps its pung's), and the value is the side of it that faces the seat it came from by the owner's screen position (the owner at the bottom: the next seat to the right, across at the top, the previous seat on the left; the other positions turn with the seat); every other slot is `None`. `BoundsOfMeld(layout, seat, meldIndex)` is the rectangle round the slots of that meld (empty if none). `HitHandTile(layout, x, y)` is the mouse's answer: the index of the viewer's concealed tile under the point, the later one where tiles overlap, `nullopt` elsewhere.

## The scene

`config/SceneConfig.xml` holds `MahjongScene` with the view size 1280 x 720 (`kViewWidth` / `kViewHeight`; the engine fits it to any window, `engine/src/Modules/BECore/Scene/CLAUDE.md`, "View size") and fourteen nodes: `Table` with `MahjongTableComponent` (`Components/`), `Hints` (layer 5, `MahjongHintsComponent` and its canvas: the waiting row and the advice, see "The hints"), `HintsButton` (layer 6, the `HintButton` on the table, a `SceneView` canvas), `Actions` (layer 10, the action buttons), `ActionsCompact` (layer 10, the same buttons and a `HintButton` in the narrow windows, a `Screen` canvas), `Hud` (layer 10, `MahjongHudComponent` and the canvas with the labels of the round, wall, turn and scores), `HudMenu` (layer 10, the New game and Rules buttons, a `Screen` canvas), `Handoff` (layer 20, the hot-seat cover), `HandOver` and `MatchOver` (layer 25, the end screens), `HandOverAction` (layer 26, the action bar with the `Next hand` button, a `Screen` canvas) `Setup` (layer 30, the seat choice), `Rules` (layer 35, the modal pages of the rules, text and tile pictures) and `RulesAction` (layer 36, their action bar with `Back`, `Next` and `Close`, a `Screen` canvas). All of it is built: the table drawn and played by mouse, the action buttons, the computers' pace, the cover, the HUD, the seat choice and the end screens.

`MahjongTableComponent::OnDraw` asks `MahjongGame::GetActive()` (null, or a match not started: the felt and the rim only), builds a `TableInput` from the model, lays it out (`MakeTableLayout`, for the view size of the scene) and draws, in this order: the felt, the rim (four rects of 8 units along the edge of the view, `kFeltRim`; drawn also with no game), the plates (`kZonePlate`, the four discard blocks and the wall ring, each 4 units wider on every side, only with a match), a highlight behind the hand of the seat whose turn it is (`HandModel::CurrentSeat()`, while `Playing`: in a claim window the discarder, never `Actor()`, which is the first seat with a claim and would show a person that this computer holds one), then every slot of the layout: the hand tiles, the melds (a chow runs up from its first tile), the bonus tiles, the discards (the one that waits for a claim has a frame) and the wall stacks (backs); every tile but a wall stack has a shadow (`kTileShadow`, the tile's rect 2 units down and right) drawn just before it (under the frame of the pending discard, so the gold is even). **The marks of the last move** (all from public data, gold family): the last discard (the entry of `Discards()` that is `Moves().back()` when that is a `Discard`; a claim, a kong or the next discard moves the mark on or removes it) has a tint `kLastDiscardMark` over its face (shadow, face, tint; the frame of a pending discard stays and shows besides it); the claimed tile of an open set (`TileSlot::claimEdge`, from the layout, so an upgraded kong keeps the mark of its pung) has a bar of `kClaimedEdgeWidth` units in `kClaimedEdge` along the edge that faces the seat it came from, drawn after the face; and the tile the viewer has just drawn (`HandModel::DrawnTile`, found as the last copy of that tile in the sorted hand) lies apart at the end of the row (`TableInput::drawnIndex`, `kDrawnTileGap`) and is discarded by a click like any other tile. The drawn tile lies apart only in a hand that is face up and while `Playing` (under the cover, for a spectator and for an opponent's hand the row is plain, so the gap tells nothing). **The set just laid blinks:** the component counts the entries of `Moves()` it has seen (`FollowMoves`, every `OnDraw`; a new hand, match or game starts over with no highlight) and the last unseen entry that made a set (`meld >= 0`: a claimed chow, pung or kong, a concealed kong, an upgraded kong) starts a highlight of `kClaimHighlightSeconds` (2.0 s) on that set, so a computer that claims and discards in one pause still shows the set it laid; while it lasts the rect round the set (`BoundsOfMeld`, 4 units wider on every side) is filled with `kClaimHighlight` behind its tiles (after the turn mark, before the slots), on for the first half of each `kClaimBlinkSeconds` (0.5 s; on at the moment of the move). The time runs on `SceneUpdateEvent::deltaTime` also under the hot-seat cover (the clock does not wait; the highlight reads public data only) and not while `Setup` is open; a later set restarts it, and it changes no game state or layout. The look and the length are the owner's (one place: `UiPalette.h`). `MahjongTableMarksTest` holds the marks and the highlight. The colours are the named constants of `Table/UiPalette.h`. The tiles are `DrawTexture` calls with `SourceRect(tile)` or `BackRect()` of the atlas `assets/tiles/tiles.png`, which the component loads once in `OnAttached`; without it (the file did not load, or a test did not hand one with `SetAtlasTexture`) they are coloured rectangles and the layout is the same. The sprite names of `TextureLibrary.xml` are not used to draw: the cells come by arithmetic (`TileAtlas.h`). `GetLayout()` is the layout of the last draw. The component draws only: it never changes the game or the session.

The seat at the bottom is the viewer (`TableSession::Viewer()`), else the person that waits for the cover to go, else the first person, else seat 0. **No peeking:** while the hand is played only the concealed tiles of the viewer are face up (its concealed kongs too), none at all while `HandoffPending()` (hot-seat cover) and none for a spectator (no person at the table); the other seats show backs, their concealed kongs too. When the hand is over (`HandOver`, `MatchOver`) all four hands are face up. Open sets, bonus tiles and discards are public. `MahjongTableUiTest` checks each slot against the model.

**Playing** (`MahjongTableComponent`, all through the session). The component subscribes with `SubscribeInScene` to `SDLEvents::MouseButtonDownEvent`, `InputEvents::ActionEvent` and `SceneEvents::SceneUpdateEvent`, so it acts only while its scene is the active one. A person can act when the phase is `Playing`, the actor is a person, no cover is up and the table is not paused (`MahjongGame::IsTablePaused()`: the seat choice `Setup` or the rules screen is open, `IsSetupOpen()` / `IsRulesOpen()`; a click then reaches the modal canvas, and an action event, a computer step and the highlight clock are dropped). *Mouse:* a left click that no canvas took (a modal canvas takes every click, so the cover blocks the table) on a tile of the viewer's hand (`HitHandTile` on the layout of the last draw, which has to be of this hand) is `PerformPerson(MakeDiscard(tile, declareNext))`; a refused action changes nothing and a click on the felt, on an opponent's tile or with another button does nothing. *Buttons:* every frame the component asks `LegalActions` of the actor (only when a person acts), turns the list into `ActionButtons` (`MakeActionButtons`) and pushes what changed: `UICanvasComponent::SetElementVisibleByName` for the element of each button (they start hidden in the file), and, for the tiles of its action, the `sprite` (`Tile_<notation>`, `SpriteName`) and the visibility of its `UIImage` children `<Element>Tile0..2` (a chow's three tiles, the pending discard on a pung / kong / mahjong claim, the tile of a concealed kong or of the upgrade; `CurrentButtons` hands `PendingTile()` to `MakeActionButtons`; a picture takes no input, so a click on it is a click on the button). Each button says a word of the string table in the file (`text="{#button.Win}"` ... `{#button.Declare}`: `Mahjong!`, `Pung`, `Kong`, `Chow`, `Pass`, `Add to pung`, `Ready hand` in English, `Маджонг!`, `Пун`, `Конг`, `Чи`, `Пас`, `Добавить`, `Заявка` in Russian); there are no label variables. The hovered button's sentence goes to the label `Hint` (see "The action buttons"). A button emits `ActionEvent(action, value)` (`MahjongWin`, `MahjongClaimMahjong`, `MahjongClaimPung`, `MahjongClaimKong`, `MahjongClaimChow` with the index 0..2 of the listed sequence, `MahjongPass`, `MahjongConcealedKong` with the index, `MahjongUpgradeKong`); the handler asks again what is on offer now and performs that button's `HandAction`, and ignores a button that is not offered, so a stale or foreign event changes nothing. The table of names is `SpecOf` (`ActionButtons.cpp`), and `MahjongTableScreenTest` compares it with the scene file. *Declare:* `MahjongDeclare` sets the flag "the next discard declares the calling hand" (`Declare` is a `UIToggle` bound to the variable `declareNext`, `on` / `off`; a click emits the state it asks for, `on` while the flag is off and `off` while it is on, `kDeclareOn` / `kDeclareOff`); the button is offered only when a listed discard declares; while the flag is on the tiles whose declaring discard is not legal are veiled and a click on them does nothing; a discard that goes through, a frame where `Declare` is no longer offered and a turn change (the acting seat or the number of discards differs from where the flag was set, e.g. the model moved on through the control API) clear the flag. *Pace:* while the phase is `Playing` and a computer acts, `SceneUpdateEvent::deltaTime` adds up and at `kComputerStepSeconds` (1.0 s; it was 0.6 s, which the owner found too fast) the component calls `StepComputer()` once and starts counting again (one step per period whatever the frame took, a claim window and the discard after it counting as one step; no counting while a person acts, the cover is up, `Setup` is open or the hand is over). A claim window is settled within one period (while the phase is `Claim` / `RobKong` and a computer acts, the component steps on without waiting) and a computer that is then to discard does so in the same period, so between computers the pause after a discard is the same whether or not a window opened (not equalised for a person: a person who acts right after a computer's discard gets the turn at once if no window opened, after the pause if one did). The control API does not wait (`RunComputers()`). *Cover:* the canvas `Handoff` (a modal, `Screen` space with `ScaleWithScreen`, hidden in the file) is shown exactly while `HandoffPending()`, with the label `{#cover.text}` ("Pass the screen to Seat 2 (South)", `handoffSeat` is the seat named in the language of the table and is set again every frame while the cover is up, so a language switch shows at the next draw) and the button `MahjongReveal` (text `{#cover.show}`, `primary`, 64 high), which calls `Reveal()` (ignored when no cover is up); the plate is 208 high: a `header` band of 96 with the 28 headline, 16, the button, 16 padding round. *A finished hand* (`HandOver`): see "The end of a hand and of a match"; the button `MahjongNextHand` calls `NextHand()`. The frame on the discard that waits for a claim is drawn only while the viewer is among `PendingClaimSeats()` (no cover): a window that only computers may claim in shows no frame.

**The HUD** (`MahjongHudComponent`, node `Hud`). *Words:* when `TableSession::Version()` (or the game) moved, the component sets `UIVars`: `round` (the name of the round wind), `hand` (the number of the hand in progress, at most `handsTotal`), `handsTotal`, `wall` (live tiles left), `turn` (`HandModel::CurrentSeat()` named `Seat 2 (South)`, never the seat that is asked to claim; `hud.handOver` / `hud.matchOver` (`Hand over` / `Match over`) after the hand / match) and per seat 0..3 `seatName` (`Seat 2 (South)`, plus ` You` for the person only when exactly one person plays), `seatWind` (the name of the wind), `seatScore`, `seatMark` (`>` on the seat whose turn it is) and `seatDealer` (`*` on East), and `lastMove` (the last accepted move of the hand in words, `MoveText` of `HandModel::Moves().back()`: `Seat 2 (South) discarded 5 Dots`, `Seat 3 (West) claimed 4 Bamboo for a chow`; empty before the first move of a hand and once the hand is over, when the end screens speak); an unchanged table sets nothing (the `UIVars` version stays) and the version of `UIStrings` joins the session version in that check, so a language switch sets them all again in the new language. The canvas texts are the keys `hud.round`, `hud.wall`, `hud.turn` (with the variables inside: `{#hud.round}` is `Round: {round}, hand {hand} of {handsTotal}`); it shows `Round: East, hand 3 of 16`, `Wall: 70`, `Turn: Seat 2 (South)` (`Info`, above the left column of melds, x 96..432, y 8..120 of the view, three rows of 32 at font 22 on a `hudPanel` plate with padding 8), the four scores `>Seat 1 (East) You*: 2000` (`Scores`, x 96..432, y 128..272, rows of 32, the same plate and right edge as `Info`: the free ground left of the top discards; it ends 4 above the left discard plate because the vertical budget does not allow 8 with seven rows of 32), and the plate `MoveLine` with the label `MoveText` (`{lastMove}`, font 18, 448 x 80 at x 740..1188, y 92..172 of the view, anchored top right with an offset of 92, on the `hudPanel` plate with padding 8; hidden in the file and shown by the component exactly while `lastMove` is not empty, so no empty plate; the ground under the top seat's sets, right of its discard plate and clear of the right seat's columns, which start at x 1196; the longest Russian sentence needs three lines at the narrowest window, hence 64 for the label). The move line is public data only (it names no tile of a concealed kong). A row of 28 does not hold the 22 px font (`CheckContent`), so the rows are 32. The HUD keeps to the safe area (the matrix demands it; the table is a fixed design that ignores it), so on a screen with a notch or side insets it moves inwards and may cover the nearest tiles; with no insets `MahjongHudTest` checks that it covers no slot of the worst-case table layout. The labels are no touch target. The button `NewGame` (`hud.newGame`, `MahjongNewGame`, 176 x 56, top right, `secondary`) is in the node `HudMenu`, a `Screen` canvas (`ScaleWithScreen`, `minScale` 0.6, `useSafeArea`; a `SceneView` button of 56 units is only 21 px high in the narrowest windows), so it is the engine's 32 px touch target in every window of the size matrix; `MahjongTouchSizeTest` checks that it covers no tile of the worst-case layout in a window without insets (with insets it lies nearer the table). The control `ui_click` finds it by name (`NewGame`, canvas `HudMenu`; the standings have a `NewGame` in `MatchOver`).
*Seat choice:* `MahjongGame::IsSetupOpen()` (`OpenSetup()`, `CloseSetup()`) says whether the modal canvas `Setup` (a `Screen` canvas, `ScaleWithScreen` with `minScale` 0.6 and no `maxScale`, hidden in the file, shown and hidden by the component every frame after the game; its dim covers the whole window, bars and the New game button of `HudMenu` included) is open. `Start()` begins a match and opens it, so the game opens on the question who plays; `StartMatch` (so a control `reset` too) and every control `PerformAction` that went through close it (what Match3 does with its `enterGame` hook: a controller that plays is in the game), and `MahjongNewGame` opens it. The plate is a `header` title band (48, 28: `kModalTitleHeight`), seven rows in `surfaceRaised` (`LanguageRow`, `SeatRow0..3`, `HandsRow`, `HintsRow`, each 64 high with 4 padding left and right: a 56 segment group (87.5 %) centred in it, a `textMuted` caption of 112 at 22) and `Start` (`primary`, 64, 16 below the rows, the empty `StartGap` makes the 16 of two 8 gaps), 664 high of the 720 of the view (the roomy shape, that of a canvas of 1280 x 720 units: with the notch of the size matrix the plate may be 669 high at most, so the title band is 48 and the rows 64, not 72; another row needs a taller view). The plate is the safe area, at most 664 high: the title, the rows and `Start` share its height by flex weights (0.75 for the title, 1 for the rest) that come out at 48 and 64 and stop growing there (`maxHeight`); a row has no vertical padding, its caption and its group are 87.5 % of its height and centred (the 4 round a 56 segment of a row of 64), so in a shorter canvas the segments follow the row. The smallest canvas of the size matrix is a 480 px window at the user scale 1.5, 320 x 320 units (262 x 297 of safe area), where a control is 32 px from 21.34 units and eight controls with the gaps leave 25 units a row, so two variants of the canvas give way there: at most 360 units high the `Title` is hidden, at most 440 wide the plate takes the whole safe width and the captions are 64 (`Language`) and 52 wide so that `Person` / `Computer` and `Человек` / `Компьютер` fit; every text shrinks (`AutoFit`, down to 12 units) instead of being cut. `MahjongHudTest` holds the words on the whole matrix (`WordsThatDoNotFitOnTheMatrix`), `MahjongTouchSizeTest` the rows, the touch size and the dim; the chosen segment is filled with `selectedColor` = `primary` (and keeps the bar), the others are `neutral`. Four `UISegmented` groups `Person` | `Computer` are bound to the variables `seatKind0..3` (`person` | `computer`) and emit `MahjongSetSeat0..3` with that value; the `Hands` group (`16` | `8`, the buttons `Hands16` / `Hands8`) is bound to `handsChoice` and emits `MahjongSetHands` (any other value is ignored, so is the action while the panel is closed). The component keeps the pending `SeatSetup` (`PendingSeats()`, taken from `GetSeats()` when the panel opens) and the pending number of hands (`PendingHands()`, taken from `GetSettings().handsPerMatch` when the panel opens; another number than 16 or 8, which only a test can set, leaves no segment selected), changes them and the variables on each click and does nothing else until `MahjongStart`, which gives both to the game (`SetSeats`, and `SetSettings` of a copy of `GetSettings()` with `handsPerMatch` set) and begins a match with a random seed (`StartRandomMatch()`); a refused start (an unplayable `handsPerMatch`, which only a test can set) leaves the panel open and the seats and settings as they were. Nothing is played while it is open: the table takes no click or button and steps no computer. The first row below the title is the language: a `UISegmented` bound to the variable `language` with one segment per language (`value` the language id, text `{#lang.en}` / `{#lang.ru}`, each written in itself) that emits `MahjongSetLanguage` with the id; the component calls `MahjongGame::SetLanguage` (which applies it to the table; the game keeps it for the session, across `New game`, `Reset()` and a control `reset`) and sets `language` from `MahjongGame::GetLanguage()` when the panel opens and after each click, so an id the table does not have changes nothing and the variable keeps the truth; a click while the panel is closed is ignored like the seat actions, and every canvas and every `UIVars` text follows at the next draw. The words of the panel are keys (`setup.title`, `setup.language`, `setup.hands`, `setup.hints`, `setup.seat1..4` for the row captions, `setup.person`, `setup.computer`, `setup.on`, `setup.off`, `setup.start`; the value of a segment is still the id `person` | `computer`); nothing the panel publishes depends on the language. The row `Hints` (`HintsRow`: a `UISegmented` bound to `hintsChoice`, the buttons `HintsOn` / `HintsOff` with `{#setup.on}` / `{#setup.off}`, value `on` | `off`) switches the hints (`MahjongGame::HintsOn()`, default on, no rule and so not in `MahjongSettings`; the row lies inside the plate of 664 units). **The hints** (`MahjongHintsComponent`, node `Hints`, `Table/Hints.h`). The button is shown exactly while hints apply and the viewer is the person to act with something to choose from (`ActorIsPerson()`, `Actor() == Viewer()`, `view.legal` not empty: also in a claim or robbing window, never while a computer acts). `MahjongHintAdvice` is ignored when the button is not offered (it asks again what is on offer now, like the action buttons); otherwise it stores `AdviceFor(view)` with the view and the table (`Key`: hints apply, viewer, `Version()`) it was asked on, and the component publishes the sentence `AdviceText(strings, view, advice)` as `hintAdvice`, shows the label and frames the advised discard on the hand (four bars of `kAdvisedFrame`, gold, 3 units, inside the edge of every slot that holds that tile; in a claim window no tile is framed). All four hands are face up under it (`HandOver`) and nothing moves behind it (no computer steps, no click). *Look* (the modal anatomy of "Setup"): the plate (`surface`, padding 16, gap 8) holds the headline in a `header` band (28, 80 high: two lines of the longest headline), the four seat rows in `surfaceRaised` (22, 32 high, centred: a label has no inner padding), the winner's lines (18, in two columns of 49%, 28 high with 4 between, 252 for the sixteen), the summary in its own `surfaceRaised` band in the muted text colour (18, 48 high) and ends with the summary band and the padding of 16; the main button `NextHand` (`primary`, 28, 64 high, 8 above and below in the 80 unit bar of `HandOverAction`) lies under the plate, at least `kModalSectionGap` below it in a window of 1280 x 720 (see "The UI style" for why it is not in the plate). `HandOverAction` has `match="1"` (the other `Screen` canvases 0.5): with 0.5 the bar is only 91-113 view units high in a 21:9 or 32:9 window and touches the plate. `MahjongNextHand` calls `TableSession::NextHand()` when the phase is still `HandOver` (a click that arrives after a control `next_hand` is out of date and ignored). A button shows its word on the top line (font 18; 22 for `Win`, `Pass` and `Declare`, which carry no tiles) and its tiles on the bottom line, 21 x 28 units each (the 3:4 of an atlas cell, small: the owner looks at them in a real window). *The hint:* above the row, the label `Hint` (x 832, y 256, 196 x 196, font 18 on the `scrim` colour, in the gutter between the wall plate and the right seat's discards: clear of every tile and plate of the table and of the row, above it; `text="{actionHint}"`, hidden in the file, no input of its own) shows the one sentence of the button under the pointer (`ButtonState::hintKey` is its key, language-free so `MakeActionButtons` stays pure; the component looks the key up with `Tr`). **The hover pattern** (`mahjong-tile-readability` can reuse it): the engine's canvas sets `UIElement::hovered` from the mouse; every frame `PublishScreen` takes the first *shown* button whose element is hovered (a picture takes no input, so the pointer on a tile picture is on the button; a hidden button may still carry the flag of the last motion and is skipped), sets the variable `actionHint` to its sentence in the current language (empty with none) and shows `Hint` exactly while it is not empty, both only when the key or the version of `UIStrings` changed (a language switch while a hint is shown changes the sentence at the next frame). At most seven are shown at once (a claim window: mahjong, kong, pung, three chows, pass; a turn: win, declare, three concealed kongs, the upgrade); `ActionsCompact` holds the hint button besides, so eight (the hints offer it in a claim window and in a turn, see "The hints"). The table is a fixed design that the engine scales to the window, so a button is a 32 px touch target from a window of 732 x 412 px (`kActionButtonHeight` * `FitScale` / density against `minTouchSize`; the boundary is 731.4 x 411.4) and smaller below it (every portrait window, 1:1, and 4:3 or 3:2 of 480 px). **Two canvases:** the rule (`NeedsCompactActions` in `Table/TouchRegime.h`) says compact where a button of `Actions` is under that minimum **and** the bar the table leaves under itself holds the compact row at its height there (`BarUnderTable` >= `CompactRowHeight(width / scale)` * `scale`, with `scale` = `CompactScale`, the scale of the compact canvas: the `ScaleWithScreen` formula at `match` 0.5 times the user scale, at least `kCompactMinScale`, and `width` the output width, so `width / scale` is the canvas width in units, which the height variants below look at; the file's settings and these constants are compared by `MahjongTableScreenTest`; the bar is taken without the safe area's inset, so a window with insets is judged like one without). The row is as high as its height at that width because a wrapped stack starts its lines at its top, whatever number of lines its buttons use, so a bar that holds one line is not enough. A window that the table fills to the bottom (a 16:9 one below 731 x 411, a short wide one like 1000 x 400, a landscape phone at density 3) has no bar, and so has one whose bar is lower than the row (600 x 420, 500 x 400, 626 x 480, and at the user scale 1.5 the windows 640 x 480 and 720 x 480 of the matrix): the compact row would lie on the viewer's hand and take the click from its tiles, so `Actions` stays there, **slightly under 32 px** (31.97 px at 731 x 411) but in the free corner, clear of the hand. Where the rule says compact, the component (`PublishScreen`, once per frame, from the output size and density of the renderer and the engine's `minTouchSize`) hides `Actions` and shows `ActionsCompact`, a `Screen` canvas (`ScaleWithScreen`, `minScale` 0.6: a 56 unit button is 33.6 px) with the same 13 elements by name, action, value, word, colour and pictures, and the `HintButton` of the hints, so the visibility and the sprites go to both by name and a click performs the same `HandAction`. A `SceneView` canvas cannot be made bigger than the table, and a `Screen` canvas cannot keep clear of the table in a 16:9 window, hence two. The compact buttons are 100 units wide (the grid of 4; the words fit at 18 and 22 in both languages; the spacing is 8) in a stack `ActionRow` at the bottom of the window, on the bar under the table; the fullest claim window is eight buttons (mahjong, kong, pung, three chows, pass and the hint button, which follows them), 856 units, and the wide `Declare` switch (180) is the last element, so that it ends a line and the fullest turn (win, three concealed kongs, the upgrade, the hint button, `Declare`: 828 units) never needs more lines than the claim window. The stack wraps, and canvas variants (`maxWidth`: 1043.91, 517.08, 385.37, 253.66, the widths of 856, 424, 316 and 208 over 0.82, rounded up, heights 120 / 184 / 248 / 504, which are 2, 3, 4 and 8 lines of 56 with 8 between; above the first the row is one line; the button width, the spacing, the count of buttons, the 82 % and these variants are `kCompactButtonWidth`, `kCompactSpacing`, `kCompactButtonCount`, `kCompactSafeShare` and `kCompactRowVariants` in `Table/TouchRegime.h`, which `CompactRowHeight` reads, and `MahjongTableScreenTest` compares them with the file and recomputes them from the arithmetic) give it the height of the lines the width needs, counted for the safe area at its narrowest (82 % of the canvas, side insets: a variant sees the whole canvas, not the safe area). The row keeps to the safe area (the validator checks the buttons against it, also where the stack has `ignoreSafeArea`), so a home bar lifts it and in a window with side insets it can lie on the nearest tiles. **Known deviations:** in the matrix that is the 3:2 window of 720 x 480 with side insets (a bar of 37.5 px, a bottom inset of 19.2 px, a line of 34.3 px) and the 1:1 windows of 480 and 720 with side insets at the user scale 1.5: the row lies on the viewer's hand by a few pixels there (never by more than the inset, because the bar holds the row), so the entry's "every window of the matrix" is met for the touch size but not for "no button over a tile" in those windows. **The UI style** (`Table/UiPalette.h` has the names, `MahjongUiStyleTest` holds `config/SceneConfig.xml` to them; an entry that draws something follows it). *Palette by role:* a control takes the colour of what it does, never a colour of its own: the table is `kFelt` with a `kFeltRim`, `kZonePlate` plates under the discard blocks and the wall ring and a `kTileShadow` under each tile; the screens are a `scrim` behind a modal (and the hint, the waiting row and the advice of the hints), a `surface` plate with a `header` band for the headline and `surfaceRaised` rows; the HUD is `hudPanel`; buttons are `primary` (confirms: Mahjong!, Start, Next hand, Show, the New game of the standings), `secondary` (claims, the HUD's New game), `neutral` (the extras of an own turn, the segments) and `danger` (Pass); text is white, `textMuted` for captions and `accent` for the first place. **The marks on the table are gold (`kTurnMark`, `kPendingFrame`, `kDeclareMark`, `kAdvisedFrame` of the hints, and the marks of the last move `kLastDiscardMark`, `kClaimedEdge`, `kClaimHighlight`; the accent text, the first standing, belongs to the same gold family) and nothing else on the table is gold**, so a later entry that marks something uses the gold family. *Type scale:* `fontSize` 18 (hint, the winner's lines, captions above tile pictures), 22 (HUD, rows, ordinary buttons), 28 (a screen's headline and its main button); no other size. *Grid:* the unit is 4 and the step 8; every `x`, `y`, `width`, `height`, `spacing` and `padding` of the file is a multiple of 4 (a percent excepted, and so are the tile pictures of the buttons, 21 x 28 units, the 3:4 of an atlas cell), so a size that `mahjong-touch-size` picks is a multiple of 4. The margin to the edge of the view is 8 (`kViewMargin`), the gap between controls 8, the padding of a HUD panel 8 and of a modal plate 16 (`kModalPadding`), the gap between the sections of a plate 16. *Anatomy of a modal screen* (`Setup`, `Handoff`, `HandOver`, `MatchOver`): the scrim, then the plate (`surface`, padding 16, rows 8 apart), the first row a headline band (`header`, 28; the standings' headline is 22, because the longest Russian line of a tie must fit the one 40-unit band of a plate of 296), rows in `surfaceRaised`, small text 18, the main button last, 64 high and 16 below the rows (a 0-high or 8-high spacer before it adds the second 8). **`HandOver` is the other place where the anatomy gives way: its main button is in the action bar `HandOverAction` of a `Screen` canvas, not in the plate, because 64 units on the view are 24 px in the smallest window and the plate cannot be a `Screen` canvas; the button keeps the role, the size and the font of a main button.** **The standings are the one place where rows are 4 apart** (`MatchOver`): the plate lies on a `Screen` canvas, which a 480 px window at the user scale 1.5 makes 320 units high (296 with a notch), so with 8 between the rows the plate would not fit and the validator would refuse it; the button is still 16 below the rows (4 + a spacer of 8 + 4). *The engine limits the look works within:* `IRenderer` has filled rectangles and textures only (no rounded corners, no border, no shadow except a rectangle drawn by hand, as the table does), the UI has one font and no weight, the colour of a label is static (it cannot follow a variable, so "this seat won" is marked by text), the selected segment is a fill plus a 4-unit bar (`selectedColor` makes the choice readable by colour). *Touch size:* every control that takes input lies in a `Screen` canvas with a `minScale` of at least 0.6 (56 units are then 33.6 px of the 32 the engine asks for), or is an action button of `Actions` / `HintsButton`, the two `SceneView` canvases that the component hides where `ActionsCompact` replaces them (`MahjongTouchSizeTest` holds this for every canvas of the file, so a new button in another `SceneView` canvas fails there). The floors are in output pixels and there is no `maxScale`; the matrix runs at pixel density 1, so a high-density phone is the owner's look (`ScaleWithScreen` follows the physical output size, which should keep a control near 32 dp on a typical phone, but only a device proves it). *Adding a control:* pick the role of what it does, a font size of the scale and sizes that are multiples of 4, write them as text in the file (`r,g,b,a` of the palette entry), run `MahjongUiStyleTest`; a new colour is a new role in `kUiPalette` and `kUiPaletteNames` and a sentence here, not a literal in the file.

## Rules for the code

- The rules live in `Game/` as plain C++ that a test drives without a scene; `MahjongGame` owns the match, components read it through `MahjongGame::GetActive()->GetMatch()`, drive it through `GetSession()`, and check that the game is the active one (`engine/src/Modules/Games/CLAUDE.md`, "Gotchas"). The seed of a match is chosen in `MahjongGame` (`std::random_device`), never in the rules.
- Components subscribe with `SubscribeInScene` (`MahjongTableComponent` does, for the mouse, the actions and the pace of the computers, `MahjongHudComponent` for the seat choice and the end screens); there is no per-frame hook in `IGame`, per-frame work belongs in a component.
- No `ImGui::` in a game component without `ImGui::GetCurrentContext()`: the HUD and the menus are UI canvases of the engine (`engine/src/Modules/BECore/UI/CLAUDE.md`).
- Every visible word is a key of `config/UIStrings.xml` in both languages: the scene says `text="{#key}"`, code reads `Tr(strings, key)`; a new text is never a literal (see "Words").
- The hints (`Table/Hints.h`, `MahjongHintsComponent`) read only a `TableView` made by `MakeTableView(hand, viewer)` for the viewer, never the `HandModel` of another seat or the live wall, and never act: no `PerformPerson`, no `Reveal`, no change of the session (see "The scene", "The hints").
- The control API is built (`MahjongGameControl`, see "Control API") and has its smoke test (`be_add_game_control_tests(BagiMahjong SCRIPT tests/control_smoke.py)` in `CMakeLists.txt`).
- New or moved sources, and every change to a file with `BE_CLASS` / `BE_REFLECT_FIELD`, need `cmake --preset <p>` again (sources are globbed, reflection is generated at configure time).

`tests/MahjongTableTestSupport.h` (header only) is the fixture of the table tests that need the scene: `MahjongTableTestBase` loads the shipped `config/SceneConfig.xml`, starts the game through `GameManager`, draws into a `RecordingRenderer` as big as the view (so a point of the view is a pixel), and offers `Start`, `Draw`, `Lay(model)` (a fixed hand in place of the one in progress), `Click`, `ClickHandTile`, `Element` / `OnScreen` / `ClickElement` for the canvases, `CoverShown`, `ExpectPassesTheMatrix` and `ExpectWordsFit`, and the free functions `EmitAction` and `Var`.

`tests/FixedWall.h` (header only) lays out a wall tile by tile for the tests of the hand: `FixedWallSpec { hands[4] (13 tiles per seat in deal order), live, loose, liveAfterDeal }`, `MakeFixedWall(spec, flowers, east = 0)` and `MakeModel(spec, east = 0, roundWind, flowers)` (a started `HandModel` on that wall); whatever the spec leaves out is padded from the set (never more than four of a kind; a spec that asks for more fails the test). Use it for every test that needs to know what a seat holds or draws.

`tests/HandTestSupport.h` (header only, namespace `BECore::Mahjong::Tests`) holds what the `HandModel` tests share: the seat names, `T("5p")`, `Error(status)`, `Has`, one `Snapshot(model)` of everything an action could change, `Lap`, the checks of `LegalActions` against `Apply`, and the random-play chooser.
