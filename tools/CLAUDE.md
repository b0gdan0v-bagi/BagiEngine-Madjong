# tools/

- `arena/main.cpp` — `BagiMahjongArena`, see `src/Mahjong/Arena/CLAUDE.md` and `docs/bot-arena.md`.
- `ui_shots.py` — seven UI scenes (`01-fresh-table` ... `07-options`) at 1920 x 1080 and scaled to 640, from `BagiMahjong --headless --control --manual-clock`. `--exe`, `--label`, `--out` (a folder outside the repository; the tool refuses one inside a git work tree). Pictures are never committed. The size is the headless window's (`SDLWindowConfig.xml`, 1920 x 1080); no control call changes it, so a **portrait** picture needs a `config/SDLWindowConfig.xml` of the game with a tall window (e.g. 720 x 1280), temporary and never committed (the game's config overrides the engine's); the shots tool does not do it.
- `make_tiles.py` — the tile art (below).
- `make_app_icons.py` — the icons of the web app (`web/icons/`, listed in `web/app.json`): the Red Dragon cell of the atlas on `kFelt`, scaled to 192 and 512. Run it after a change of the art; `--check` compares the pixels.
- `web_smoke.py` — the browser smoke of the packaged page (`engine/tools/web/package.py --game BagiMahjong`). Needs Playwright (venv `build/web-venv`, `engine/tools/web/CLAUDE.md`, "The browser smoke") and a browser (`--browser-channel chrome` needs no download). `python tools/web_smoke.py --dist dist --browser-channel chrome --expect-console "Initialized with \d+ worker threads"` (`dist-single` with `"single-thread mode"`); exit 0 / 1 / 77 / 2 as the engine's. It runs the engine's checks 1 to 5 with the click on `Start`, then a second page load: Start, wait until the table stands still, click the first tile of the hand, the picture must change. East is a dice draw, so the person may face a claim window and not be first to act: the scenario presses the places of the action buttons (`CLAIM_ANSWER_CLICKS`) when a tile click does nothing and clicks the tile again; no outcome of the first move is assumed. The coordinates are for a 1280 x 720 window (`--start-click`, `--tile-click` replace them); **when Setup changes, re-check `START_CLICK`** (`mahjong-setup-options` moves it, whoever merges second). `--check` runs the browser-free helpers; `--tile-click 5,5` must exit 1.

## Tile art

Our own art (owner decision: no third-party tile set, no font file). The script is a small software rasterizer with a 5x7 bitmap font of our own and one function per face; the art *is* the script.

- `python3 tools/make_tiles.py` writes `assets/tiles/tiles.png` and `config/TextureLibrary.xml`. **Run `--check` after every change** (compares decoded pixels and the XML, and checks the cells: all different, opaque middle, transparent corners, an index on every face and no art under it, the stack solid in its square and nothing outside it).
- `--preview OUT.png` (outside the repository) writes a contact sheet at four sizes for judging readability.
- Atlas: 8 x 6 cells of 96 x 128 (44 drawn), cell = tile id, cell 42 the back, cell 43 `Tile_Stack`: a wall stack seen from above, a `STACK_SIZE` (96) square at the top left of its cell, the rest transparent (the sprite is the square; `StackRect()`). `CELL_W`, `CELL_H`, `COLUMNS` are the one place of the geometry and must match `src/Mahjong/Table/TileAtlas.h` (`TileSpritesTest`).
- A tile is a block: the ivory side shows `SIDE_BOTTOM` / `SIDE_RIGHT` units (2.5 to 3 px at discard size) below and right of the face, in three bands (`SIDE_LIGHT`, `SIDE`, `SIDE_SHADE`); the back has the same side. The patterns are laid out in `ART_FACE` and mapped into the smaller `BODY_FACE` by `art_view`, so a change of the side width moves `ART_SCALE`, not the layouts. Never loosen `index_problems`: shrink the art.
- Every face but the back has a corner index (`INDEX_PIXEL`, `INDEX_X`, `INDEX_Y`, `INDEX_BOX`) so a tile reads at discard size (24 x 32); the art is drawn scaled by `ART_SCALE` to leave room for it. The White dragon's index is a hollow frame (a `W` would read as West).
