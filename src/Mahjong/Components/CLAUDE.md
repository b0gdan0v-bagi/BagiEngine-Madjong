# Components/ — the scene

The glue between the model and the engine's scene and UI. Pure logic lives in `../Table/` (read its `CLAUDE.md`); the node files, the string table and the UI style are in `config/` (`config/CLAUDE.md`).

- `MahjongTableComponent` (node `Table`) draws the table and plays the person seats by mouse and buttons, paces the computers, flies tiles, shows the hot-seat cover, swaps `Actions` / `ActionsCompact`. After the layout it publishes `MakeTableRegions` to the canvas of every node in `kRegionNodes` (`SetRegion`, only what changed); those nodes anchor to the regions and do not move with insets.
- `MahjongHudComponent` (node `Hud`) publishes the HUD, seat-plate and centre-panel words and runs `Setup`, the rules screen and the end screens.
- `MahjongHintsComponent` (node `Hints`) runs the hints.
- `MahjongOnlineComponent` (node `Online`) shows the online panel (`OnlineScreens`, the canvases `Online` and `OnlineEntry`) and runs the client of `MahjongGame` once per frame; `Online/CLAUDE.md`, "The screens". Escape is the panel's while `IsOnlineOpen()` (the HUD ignores it then); the `Online` button of `Setup` is hidden in the browser build.

## Rules for every component

- Subscribe with `SubscribeInScene` (mouse, `ActionEvent`, `SceneUpdateEvent`), so nothing reacts while another scene is active. Check `MahjongGame::GetActive()` and that a match is started.
- Change the game only through `TableSession` (`PerformPerson`, `StepComputer`, `NextHand`, `Reveal`). **The table component draws only from the model; hints never act** (no `PerformPerson`, no `Reveal`, no version change).
- A button handler asks again what is on offer *now* and ignores an event that is not offered (stale clicks, events after a control action).
- Publish `UIVars` only when something changed: the key is `TableSession::Version()` plus the version of `UIStrings` (a language switch rewords everything at the next draw). An unchanged frame must not move the `UIVars` version (tests check it).
- `IsTablePaused()` (`Setup`, `Options`, rules or the online panel open) stops everything on the table: clicks, action events, computer steps, the blink and flight clocks, the hints.
- Every word is a key (`text="{#key}"` in the scene, `Tr` in code).

## The table (`MahjongTableComponent`)

**Draw order:** felt (the texture of `Table/TableLook.h`, flat `kFelt` when the renderer gives none), rim and its lip (also with no game; no box under the discards or the wall), the centre panel (ground, the turn light, the dealer mark), seat plates (each with its emblem and rings), the rack under the viewer's hand (`kHandRackLip` plate `kRackPadding` past the hand, ends at the rim, `kHandRack` on it; public, so for a spectator and under the cover too; wood, never gold), turn backdrop of `CurrentSeat()` (in a claim window the discarder, never `Actor()`), the highlight of a set just laid, calling frames, then every slot (a two-layer shadow before each tile, none under a wall stack), then flying tiles in a second pass. Tiles are `DrawTexture` from the atlas (a wall stack from `StackRect()`), a slot with a `rotation` `DrawTransformedTexture` of `UprightRect(slot.rect)` about the centre of `slot.rect`. Shadows and every mark are `DrawFilledRect` on the **screen** rectangle of the slot. `GetLayout()` is the layout of the last draw. Each draw builds it for the **active view** of the scene (`GetScene().GetViewWidth/Height()`: the design of one of four shapes by the aspect of the output, `Table/CLAUDE.md`) with the profile `TableProfileFor` gives the window (renderer output size, UI pixel density, `minTouchSize`, the view: a landscape phone gets the large one-row hand); the rim, lip and shadows scale with `LayoutScale` of the view; `NeedsCompactActions` and the tap regime read the view too.

**Table layout spec:** the zones come from the active `TableLayoutSpec`, which `OnAttached` fills from the config `TableLayout` (the tests set it from the source tree). `OnDraw` polls every `kTableLayoutPollSeconds` of wall-clock time (the table is drawn while paused or under a modal): a changed file stamp gives `ReloadConfig`, then `OfferTableLayoutSpec` on the cached document (also with an unchanged stamp, so an unsaved Config Editor edit shows). A spec that does not read or fails `CheckTableLayoutSpec` is refused, the last good one stays, the errors are logged once per distinct text. A file that cannot be stat'ed (browser) is silent.

**No peeking:** while playing only the viewer's concealed tiles are face up; none under the hot-seat cover; all four once the hand is over **and while no seat is a person** (a watched match; the computers still see `MakeTableView` only). The marks follow the viewer (`Faces::seat`), not the hands face up, so a spectator has none. Bottom seat = `BottomSeat(session)`.

**Marks** (all from public data; gold unless said otherwise, see `config/CLAUDE.md`): last discard tint; claimed-edge bar (`claimEdge`); drawn-tile tint (only a face-up hand while playing); pending-discard frame (only while the viewer is in `PendingClaimSeats()`); the **chow frame** on the two concealed tiles an offered chow takes (`ChowTilesTaken`: the hovered `Chow` button's, else the only chow on offer; only a face-up hand of the person to act); the blink of a set just laid (`kClaimHighlightSeconds`, waits for its tiles to land, keeps running under the cover); the calling frame of every declared hand (reads the flag only); the **centre panel** (public: ground, a gold light on the edge that faces `TurnSeat`, a red mark at the dealer's cell, read from the winds of the hand shown; `PanelTurnLight` / `PanelDealerMark`); the **wind bars** on the viewer's hand tiles that are its seat wind (blue) or the round wind (violet; both: left half and right half), not gold, only a face-up hand while playing (`WindMarkOf`); the keeps-waiting bars of the viewer's declared hand on its own discard turn (**not tied to the Hints switch**, owner 2026-11-09); **a claim that lost**: after a window with a `beaten` claim (`LastClaimWindow()`, followed by its `serial` with the restart rule of the moves) the winner's plate blinks and every beaten seat's plate gets the `kClaimLostFrame` for `kClaimLostSeconds`.

**Flights:** each draw keeps a `TableSnapshot`; all moves since the previous draw are one batch planned by `PlanFlights`. A flight carries no face: the slot is drawn by the same `drawSlot` at `FlightRect`, so a back flies as a back and the slot's marks travel with it. The face turns from the rotation of its source slot to its own by the shortest way (`FlightPlacement`); the marks stay on the screen rectangle. A win is no move, so the snapshot carries it (`winSeat` / `winIndex`): a mahjong on a discard flies from its pile slot into the winner's hand. A new batch lands the running flights; a new hand / match / game (`StartCount()` too), another viewer, shape or view size start with none. `SetTileFlightSeconds(0)` turns them off; `MahjongTableTestBase` does, only `MahjongTableFlightTest` turns them on.

**Playing:** a person acts when `Playing`, the actor is a person, no cover, not paused.
- *Mouse:* a left click no canvas took, on a viewer's hand tile (`HitHandTile`) = `PerformPerson(MakeDiscard(tile, declareNext))`.
- *Hover name:* the pointer over a **face-up** tile (`TileOfSlot`, the draw's own rule; never a back, a wall stack or a tile in flight) words `tileHoverText` (`TileName`), on a viewer hand tile with a danger bar also `tileHoverDanger` (`DangerHoverKey`, the plate grows), and shows the plate `TileHoverPlate` of the node `TileHover` below it (`HoverPlateRect`; the table moves it through `style.rect` + `Invalidate` only when its place changed). The table **forgets** the pointer when paused, under the cover, on a touch or a pen, and while a canvas that takes input is modal or shows a `hovered` element (`CanvasHasPointer`).
- *Tap regime* (`UsesTapToSelect`, `Table/TapSelect.h`): once a click SDL made from a touch or a pen was seen (`_touchSeen`, sticky), or while a hand tile is under `minTouchSize`, `StepTap` makes the first click on a tile a **selection** (the tile is drawn at `RaisedRect` after every other slot so the bonus row does not cover it, all its marks and the hints' declare bar and advice frame follow, `SelectedHandIndex()`); the same tile again discards, another moves it, a click on no tile clears it. The selection is kept as index + tile + turn + session version + hand, and dropped when any differs, in a claim window, under the cover, paused or with the regime off; the `Declare` toggle and a refused discard keep it. `selectedTileText` (`SelectedTileText`) names it, empty with none. The control API never sees the selection.
- *Buttons:* each frame `MakeActionButtons(LegalActions(actor))` → show/hide elements and set the tile pictures **by name** (so `Actions` and `ActionsCompact` both follow). Buttons emit `ActionEvent` (`MahjongWin`, `MahjongClaimPung`, `MahjongClaimChow` + index, `MahjongPass`, ...; the table is `SpecOf`).
- *Declare:* `MahjongDeclare` toggles "the next discard declares"; while on, non-declaring tiles are veiled and ignored; a discard, a frame where it is no longer offered, or a turn change clears it.
- *Pace:* one `StepComputer()` per `kComputerStepSeconds` (the owner's pace at Normal); the player's speed (`GetAnimationSpeed()`, `Table/AnimationSpeed.h`) scales that, the flight time and the claim marks together (`Seconds()`); a claim window and the discard after it are one step. **A search seat thinks in the background** (`ComputerThinking`, `../CLAUDE.md`): the component begins the step (`BeginComputerStep`) at the start of the pause and applies it (`FinishComputerStep`) at the pause end, or when the answer arrives if the search is slower, never earlier; a decision that needs no search is applied at the pause end as ever, with no task. The step is dropped, with nothing applied, when no computer acts any more (a person, the hand or the match over, `IsTablePaused()`, the game no longer active), when the session `Version()` or `StartCount()` moved (control action, new match, `Reveal`; the next frame begins again, the pause already spent stays) and with the component. With the manual clock the search is run to its end inside the frame.
- *Cover:* `Handoff` is shown exactly while `HandoffPending()`; its button calls `Reveal()`.

**Action buttons:** two canvases hold the same 13 buttons by name (`MahjongTableScreenTest` compares them), and the component shows one (`NeedsCompactActions`, which counts the bottom inset; `TouchRegimeTest`). `Actions` is a `SceneView` canvas: `ActionRow` fills the region `table.actions`, the `Hint` label `table.hint`; the sizes per shape are `ActionButtonHeightFor` and the node's variants. `ActionsCompact` is a `Screen` canvas with `minScale` on the bar under the table, plus a `HintButton`; `Actions` and `HintsButton` hide then. **Why two, the engine's lack and not a taste:** a `SceneView` canvas has the table's fit as its scale (no floor), and a variant sees view units, not output pixels, so no variant of `Actions` can make a button bigger in a small window; one canvas of regions for both would also hold a different number of buttons per line (the canvas scale differs from the fit). A scale floor for a `SceneView` canvas would make them one. `Actions` shows where its buttons are 32 px or more, or where the bar under the table cannot hold the compact row above the safe area (it would lie on the hand). **Deviations** (`ActionsMayBeSmall` names them; the clear-of-hand check holds everywhere): the 1:1 portrait window of 480 px (25.5 px, no bar), the near-square narrow window with side insets (610 x 600: 31.6 px). The `Hint` label shows the sentence of the hovered shown button of `Actions` (none on the compact row: touch has no hover).

## The HUD (`MahjongHudComponent`)

- **Words:** `lastMove` (`MoveText` of the last move; empty before the first move and once the hand is over; the `MoveLine` plate is shown exactly while it has text; the plate `SelectedTile` of the same node says `selectedTileText`, shown by the table component, not the HUD), and per screen position `p` `plateName<p>`, `panelWind<p>` (one letter), `panelScore<p>`, plus `panelRound` and `panelWall` (`MakeCenterPanel`). There is no turn word: the turn is the gold of a seat plate and the light of the centre panel.
- **Seat plates:** four plates of one size at the sides of the table (`zones.seatPlates`); the table component paints them (base, turn tint and frame, a red dealer bar, the emblem of the seat in `EmblemBox` with a turn ring and a calling ring tinted by palette roles), `SeatPlates` labels carry the name only, by fractions of the region `table.plate.<position>`, under the emblem. Public data: shown under the cover and at a hand's end.
- **Centre panel:** the table paints its ground, turn light and dealer mark (see Marks); the node `CenterPanel` words the round and hand (`East 1`), the wall and, on the side of each seat, its wind letter and points, by fractions of the region `table.centerPanel` (the inside of the wall ring); labels only, by screen position and upright for every side (no label turn in the engine).
- **`HudMenu`:** `New game` and `Options` in a `Screen` canvas (touch targets), at the window's top right corner; its own variants (portrait, narrow) put them in the table's free ground. It names no table region on purpose: its canvas scale differs from the table's fit and its landscape places are corners of the window, so a region cannot hold them (`MahjongHudTest`, `MahjongTouchSizeTest`). The other `Screen` canvases (`Setup`, `Options`, `MatchOver`, `Handoff`, `Rules*`, `HandOverAction`) are centred plates or corner buttons on the grid and carry no table number. Hidden while the score table or the rules are up.
- **Save:** each frame (`SceneUpdateEvent`, active game only) the HUD calls `MahjongGame::FlushSave()` (`../Save/CLAUDE.md`).
- **`Setup`** (modal `Screen` canvas): four seats, each with a level `UIDropdown` (`1` .. `10`, `MahjongSetSeatLevelN`) shown exactly while that seat is a computer in the pending setup, hands 16 | 8, `Start`, and `Continue` beside it (half the row each) exactly while `CanContinue()`; `MahjongContinue` calls `ContinueMatch()` and plays nothing by itself. The component keeps the pending choices (seats, levels, taken from the game when the panel opens; a person's seat keeps its level) and applies them only on `Start` (`SetSeats`, `SetLevels`, `handsPerMatch` of a copy of the settings, `StartRandomMatch()`; a refused start puts all back). The close cross `SetupClose` (right end of the title band `TitleRow`, 56 high for touch) and Escape (`SDLEvents::KeyDownEvent`, not a repeat) call `CloseSetup()`: no start, no pending choice applied, the table dealt or running behind the panel is where the player was. It never changes `flowers`, `replayDrawnHand`, `limit` or `startingScore`. Events while closed are ignored.
- **`Options`** (modal `Screen` canvas, layer 31, the table paused): language, hints off | hints | coach (Coach includes them), speed slow | normal | fast (`MahjongSetSpeed`, `speedChoice`: the pace, flights and claim marks), `Rules` and `Back`. The HUD button opens it unless `Setup` or the rules are up; `Back`, Escape and `CloseSetup()` close it; `Rules` swaps it for the rules. Its three actions (`MahjongSetHints` is `MahjongHintsComponent`'s) apply at once and only while it is open.
- **Rules screen** (from `Options`): `Rules` (modal `SceneView`, text and tile pictures) plus the bar `RulesAction` (`Screen` canvas, Back / Next / Close: in the plate 64 view units are 24 px in the smallest window). The page is clamped, 0 whenever closed; `FormatRulesPage` is published when the page, the open state or the language moved. A new match and any accepted control action close it.
- **End screens:** `HandOver` (modal `SceneView`, text only, **no dim**: all four hands stay face up and nothing of the plate covers a hand, meld or bonus tile, but in the landscape phone profile it still covers the viewer's melds and bonus tiles: left for the owner; the plate is sized in percents of the view, with a variant for portrait (lines in two columns, between the side seats' bonus columns), narrow and wide) with `HandOverAction` (the `Next hand` / `Finish match` button, a `Screen` canvas over the plate's gap, in the middle of the window, in both shapes). The hand is settled for the screen with `SettleHand(Hand())`; scores move only on `NextHand`. More than 16 lines show 15 and `+n more`. **One hold:** when a person's claim lost to a mahjong (any winner) in a window the table component settled itself (a click or a pace step), `HandOver` and `HandOverAction` wait while `MahjongTableComponent::HandOverHeld()`, so the plate does not cover the flying tile and the marks; the words are published at once, and a window settled by a control client holds nothing. `MatchOver` (`Screen` canvas) shows the standings and its `New game` opens `Setup`. In Coach mode, once the match tally has a move, each plate gets one tally row (`HandCoachRow`, `MatchCoachRow`, `FormatCoachTally`) paid for inside the fixed plate heights. A top-notch window pushes the corner button onto the plate's top padding: accepted (owner's call).

## The hints (`MahjongHintsComponent`)

Apply when `HintsApply(HintsOn(), IsTablePaused(), session)`: mode Hints or Coach, not paused, `Playing`, the viewer a person, no cover. Never for a spectator or once the hand is over; the control API does not know them (`get_state` is equal in the three modes).

- **Waiting row** in the free region `table.waiting` (the hint button and the advice lie in `table.hintButton` / `table.advice`): the tiles the viewer's hand waits for with the unseen copies (`WaitingTiles`), while the hand has 3n+1 tiles. Recomputed only when the version, viewer or `HintsApply` changed.
- **Declare marks:** gold bars under the hand tiles whose discard declares (`DeclaringDiscards`), on the viewer's turn. **Danger marks** while an opponent has declared: a green / amber / red bar (safe / risky / dangerous, `DangerMarks`) inside the left edge, between the wind bar and the keeps-waiting bar.
- **Advice:** `HintButton` (node `HintsButton`, or in `ActionsCompact`) is offered while the viewer is the person to act, and is a **switch** (the component's state, kept across turns, hands and matches, not saved; both buttons drawn `selected` while on). While on, every table the viewer acts on gets `AdviceFor(view)` by itself, once per table, and the advised tile is framed; in Hints the sentence is the label `AdviceText` (`hintAdvice`, top left). Any version or viewer change, hints off, pause or cover drops it; the switch brings it back on the next table. It never acts.
- **The card of the coach** (mode Coach; owner 2026-11-12: one place, not three): `CoachCard` of the node `HintsWhy` (layer 11) fills the wall ring, covering the centre panel and its turn light; no input (a press inside takes the advice down, read in `OnMouseDown`; `CardClose` says so). It says the ledger's **note** while one is up (this hand and viewer, `MakeNoteCard`), else the **advice** while the switch is on (`MakeAdviceCard`), the tally last; in Coach `AdviceText` stays hidden. Published only when `CardKey` moved (advice or note, language, tally); its variables are emptied when it goes down. The note goes on the next accepted action, a press it was shown for, `Hint`, hand end, new match, pause, cover or a mode change.
