# Components/ — the scene

The glue between the model and the engine's scene and UI. Pure logic lives in `../Table/` (read its `CLAUDE.md`); the node files, the string table and the UI style are in `config/` (`config/CLAUDE.md`).

- `MahjongTableComponent` (node `Table`) draws the table and plays the person seats by mouse and buttons, paces the computers, flies tiles, shows the hot-seat cover, swaps `Actions` / `ActionsCompact`.
- `MahjongHudComponent` (node `Hud`) publishes the HUD, seat-plate and centre-panel words and runs `Setup`, the rules screen and the end screens.
- `MahjongHintsComponent` (node `Hints`) runs the hints.

## Rules for every component

- Subscribe with `SubscribeInScene` (mouse, `ActionEvent`, `SceneUpdateEvent`), so nothing reacts while another scene is active. Check `MahjongGame::GetActive()` and that a match is started.
- Change the game only through `TableSession` (`PerformPerson`, `StepComputer`, `NextHand`, `Reveal`). **The table component draws only from the model; hints never act** (no `PerformPerson`, no `Reveal`, no version change).
- A button handler asks again what is on offer *now* and ignores an event that is not offered (stale clicks, events after a control action).
- Publish `UIVars` only when something changed: the key is `TableSession::Version()` plus the version of `UIStrings` (a language switch rewords everything at the next draw). An unchanged frame must not move the `UIVars` version (tests check it).
- `IsTablePaused()` (`Setup`, `Options` or rules open) stops everything on the table: clicks, action events, computer steps, the blink and flight clocks, the hints.
- Every word is a key (`text="{#key}"` in the scene, `Tr` in code).

## The table (`MahjongTableComponent`)

**Draw order:** felt (the texture of `Table/TableLook.h`, flat `kFelt` when the renderer gives none; made once per `IRenderer::GetId()`, freed with the GPU idle on clean-up, a renderer change and the component's end), rim and its lip (also with no game; no box under the discards or the wall), the centre panel (ground, the turn light, the dealer mark), seat plates, the rack under the viewer's hand (`kHandRackLip` plate `kRackPadding` past the hand, ends at the rim, `kHandRack` on it; public, so for a spectator and under the cover too; wood, never gold), turn backdrop of `CurrentSeat()` (in a claim window the discarder, never `Actor()`), the highlight of a set just laid, calling frames, then every slot (a two-layer shadow before each tile, the wide faint `kTileShadowSoft` then the tight `kTileShadow` at an offset, none under a wall stack), then flying tiles in a second pass. Tiles are `DrawTexture` from the atlas (a wall stack from `StackRect()`, the stack cell), a slot with a `rotation` (the side seats) `DrawTransformedTexture` of `UprightRect(slot.rect)` about the centre of `slot.rect`; without the atlas, coloured rectangles in the same layout (`DrawTransformedRect` for a turned slot). Shadows (the soft layer reaches `kShadowSoftMargin` units past the tight one) and every mark are `DrawFilledRect` on the **screen** rectangle of the slot. `GetLayout()` is the layout of the last draw. Each draw builds it for the **active view** of the scene (`GetScene().GetViewWidth/Height()`: the design of one of four shapes by the aspect of the output, `Table/CLAUDE.md`) with the profile `TableProfileFor` gives the window (renderer output size, UI pixel density, `minTouchSize`, the view: a landscape phone gets the large one-row hand); the rim, lip and shadows scale with `LayoutScale` of the view, `NeedsCompactActions` and the tap regime read the view too. A changed profile, shape or view size starts the tiles with no flight. The felt is one texture stretched over any view.

**Table layout spec:** the zones come from the active `TableLayoutSpec` (`ActiveTableLayoutSpec`), which `OnAttached` fills from the config `TableLayout` when the game has it (the tests have none and set it from the source tree). `OnDraw` polls every `kTableLayoutPollSeconds` of wall-clock time (not scene time: the table is drawn while paused or under a modal): the file stamp changed gives `ReloadConfig`, then `OfferTableLayoutSpec` on the cached document (also with an unchanged stamp, so an unsaved Config Editor edit shows). A spec that does not read or fails `CheckTableLayoutSpec` is refused, the last good one stays, the errors are logged once per distinct text. A file that cannot be stat'ed (browser) is silent. No `XmlNode` is kept across a poll.

**No peeking:** while playing only the viewer's concealed tiles are face up; none under the hot-seat cover and none for a spectator; all four once the hand is over. Bottom seat = `BottomSeat(session)`.

**Marks** (all from public data; gold unless said otherwise, see `config/CLAUDE.md`): last discard tint; claimed-edge bar (`claimEdge`); drawn-tile tint (only a face-up hand while playing); pending-discard frame (only while the viewer is in `PendingClaimSeats()`); the **chow frame** on the two concealed tiles an offered chow takes (`ChowTilesTaken`: the hovered `Chow` button's, else the only chow on offer, else none; same gold frame, only a face-up hand of the person to act, none under the cover); the blink of a set just laid (`kClaimHighlightSeconds`, waits for its tiles to land, keeps running under the cover); the calling frame of every declared hand (reads the flag only); the **centre panel** (public: the ground in the plate colour, a gold light on the edge that faces `TurnSeat`, so none unless `Playing` and on the discarder in a claim window, and a red mark on the inner boundary of the cell of the dealer, read from the winds of the hand shown; the rectangles are `PanelTurnLight` / `PanelDealerMark`); the **wind bars** on the viewer's hand tiles that are its seat wind (blue) or the round wind (violet; both: left half and right half), not gold, only a face-up hand while playing (`WindMarkOf`); the keeps-waiting bars of the viewer's declared hand on its own discard turn (**not tied to the Hints switch**, owner 2026-11-09); **a claim that lost**: after a window with a `beaten` claim (`LastClaimWindow()`, followed by its `serial` with the restart rule of the moves) the winner's plate blinks and every beaten seat's plate gets the `kClaimLostFrame` for `kClaimLostSeconds`. A pass is no claim; a window with one claimer or none marks nothing.

**Flights:** each draw keeps a `TableSnapshot`; all moves since the previous draw are one batch planned by `PlanFlights`. A flight carries no face: the slot is drawn by the same `drawSlot` at `FlightRect`, so a back flies as a back and the slot's marks travel with it. The face turns from the rotation of its source slot to that of its own by the shortest way (`FlightPlacement`; 0 and 270 meet through 315), so a tile into or out of a side seat turns on the way and lands as its slot; the marks stay on the screen rectangle. A win is no move, so the snapshot carries it (`winSeat` / `winIndex`): a mahjong on a discard flies from its pile slot into the winner's hand. A new batch lands the running flights; a new hand / match / game (`StartCount()` too), another viewer, shape or view size start with none. `SetTileFlightSeconds(0)` turns them off; `MahjongTableTestBase` does, only `MahjongTableFlightTest` turns them on.

**Playing:** a person acts when `Playing`, the actor is a person, no cover, not paused.
- *Mouse:* a left click no canvas took, on a viewer's hand tile (`HitHandTile`) = `PerformPerson(MakeDiscard(tile, declareNext))`.
- *Tap regime* (`UsesTapToSelect`, `Table/TapSelect.h`): once a click SDL made from a touch or a pen was seen (`_touchSeen`, sticky), or while a hand tile is under `minTouchSize`, `StepTap` makes the first click on a tile a **selection** (the tile is drawn at `RaisedRect` after every other slot so the bonus row does not cover it, all its marks and the hints' declare bar and advice frame follow, `SelectedHandIndex()`); the same tile again discards, another moves it, a click on no tile clears it. The hit region and `GetLayout()` stay the unraised ones. The selection is kept as index + tile + turn + session version + hand, and dropped on the next frame or click when any of them differs, in a claim window, under the cover, paused or with the regime off; the `Declare` toggle keeps it, and a refused discard (a tile that cannot declare) keeps it. Nothing is selected in a claim window. `selectedTileText` (`SelectedTileText`, `table.selectedTile`) names it, empty with none. The control API never sees the selection.
- *Buttons:* each frame `MakeActionButtons(LegalActions(actor))` → show/hide elements and set the tile pictures **by name** (so `Actions` and `ActionsCompact` both follow). Buttons emit `ActionEvent` (`MahjongWin`, `MahjongClaimPung`, `MahjongClaimChow` + index, `MahjongPass`, ...; the table is `SpecOf`).
- *Declare:* `MahjongDeclare` toggles "the next discard declares"; while on, non-declaring tiles are veiled and ignored; a discard, a frame where it is no longer offered, or a turn change clears it.
- *Pace:* one `StepComputer()` per `kComputerStepSeconds` (the owner's pace at Normal); the player's speed (`GetAnimationSpeed()`, `Table/AnimationSpeed.h`) scales that, the flight time and the claim marks together (`Seconds()`); a claim window and the discard after it are one step. The control API does not wait. **A search seat thinks in the background** (`ComputerThinking`, `../CLAUDE.md`): the component begins the step (`BeginComputerStep`) at the start of the pause and applies it (`FinishComputerStep`) at the pause end, or when the answer arrives if the search is slower, never earlier; a decision that needs no search is applied at the pause end as ever, with no task. Each later step of a claim window burst is begun as the one before is applied. The step is dropped, with nothing applied, when no computer acts any more (a person, the hand or the match over, `IsTablePaused()`, the game no longer active), when the session `Version()` or `StartCount()` moved (control action, new match, `Reveal`; the next frame begins again, the pause already spent stays) and with the component. With the manual clock the search is run to its end inside the frame. No level turns the search on yet, so a started game has no such seat.
- *Cover:* `Handoff` is shown exactly while `HandoffPending()`; its button calls `Reveal()`.

**Action buttons:** `Actions` is a `SceneView` canvas in free ground of the table (landscape: the free corner, buttons 56 high; wide: the same a wing in; narrow: 88 wide in two lines right of the bottom discards, with the `Hint` label left of them; portrait: one row between the viewer's discards and plate, 68 high, `ActionButtonHeightFor`); a button is a 32 px touch target from a 732 x 412 landscape window, from 617 px wide in the narrow view, from 339 px wide in portrait. Where `NeedsCompactActions` says so (a button under the minimum **and** the bar under the table holds the compact row at its height), `Actions` and `HintsButton` hide and `ActionsCompact` (a `Screen` canvas at the bottom, the same 13 elements plus a `HintButton`) shows. A window with no bar for the row keeps `Actions` with buttons slightly under 32 px but clear of the hand (in landscape the row ends above the viewer's melds of the phone profile, `Table/CLAUDE.md`), so every canvas on the table is checked against the layout of its window (`ForEachTableWindow`). The `Hint` label shows the sentence of the hovered shown button of `Actions` (none on the compact row: touch has no hover). **Known deviations** (the owner's call; named in `ActionsMayBeSmall`): the 1:1 window of 480 px in portrait keeps small `Actions` (no bar: the table fills it); `ActionsCompact` is only for portrait windows under 339 px and nearly square narrow ones (610 x 600), and with side insets there it overlaps the hand by up to the inset (an engine change to `ignoreSafeArea` for descendants would fix it).

## The HUD (`MahjongHudComponent`)

- **Words:** `lastMove` (`MoveText` of the last move; empty before the first move and once the hand is over; the `MoveLine` plate is shown exactly while it has text; the plate `SelectedTile` of the same node says `selectedTileText`, shown by the table component, not the HUD), and per screen position `p` `plateName<p>`, `panelWind<p>` (one letter), `panelScore<p>`, plus `panelRound` and `panelWall` (`MakeCenterPanel`). There is no turn word: the turn is the gold of a seat plate and the light of the centre panel.
- **Seat plates:** four plates of one size at the sides of the table (`zones.seatPlates`); the table component paints them (base, turn tint and frame, a red dealer bar), `SeatPlates` labels carry the name only. Public data, so shown under the cover and at the end of a hand. The dealer is read from the winds of the hand shown, not `Match::East()`.
- **Centre panel:** the table paints its ground, turn light and dealer mark (see Marks); the node `CenterPanel` words the round and hand (`East 1`), the wall and, on the side of each seat, its wind letter and points, inside `zones.centerPanel` (the inside of the wall ring); labels only, by screen position and upright for every side (no label turn in the engine), on the grid inside the panel. A 2D overlay: the 3D table (`mahjong-table-3d`) is not touched.
- **`HudMenu`:** `New game` and `Rules` in a `Screen` canvas (touch targets); in portrait a variant moves them to the free ground left of the viewer's discards (the top right corner is the top seat's bonus row there), in a narrow window of aspect 1.1 and more to the bottom left corner (`MahjongHudTest` / `MahjongTouchSizeTest` hold them clear of tiles). Hidden while the score table or the rules are up.
- **Save:** each frame (`SceneUpdateEvent`, active game only) the HUD calls `MahjongGame::FlushSave()` (`../Save/CLAUDE.md`).
- **`Setup`** (modal `Screen` canvas): four seats, each with a level `UIDropdown` (`1` .. `10`, `MahjongSetSeatLevelN`) shown exactly while that seat is a computer in the pending setup, hands 16 | 8, the full-width button `Options`, `Start`, and `Continue` beside it (half the row each) exactly while `CanContinue()`; `MahjongContinue` calls `ContinueMatch()` and plays nothing by itself. The component keeps the pending choices (seats, levels, taken from the game when the panel opens; a person's seat keeps its level) and applies them only on `Start` (`SetSeats`, `SetLevels`, `handsPerMatch` of a copy of the settings, `StartRandomMatch()`; a refused start puts all back). It offers nothing else and never changes `flowers`, `replayDrawnHand`, `limit` or `startingScore`. Events while closed are ignored. Layout variants: canvases ≤ 480 wide (narrow captions, the plate takes the safe width) and ≤ 360 wide (the seat captions go: Person / Computer, the dropdown and the plate's padding leave no room for them); the title stays in every window. A dropdown header is no label: the word tests do not see it, `MahjongHudTest` checks it on the matrix with level 10.
- **`Options`** (modal `Screen` canvas, layer 31, over `Setup`): language, hints on | off, the speed (`UISegmented` slow | normal | fast, `MahjongSetSpeed`, variable `speedChoice`; scales the pace, the flights and the claim marks, at once; the narrow variant hides its caption), `Back` (the level is on `Setup`, per seat). `MahjongOptions` opens it only while `Setup` is open; `Setup` stays open under it, so the pending seats and hands and `Continue` survive; `CloseSetup()` closes it. `MahjongSetLanguage`, `MahjongSetHints` (`MahjongHintsComponent`) and `MahjongSetSpeed` are accepted only while it is open and apply at once. Two layout variants (≤ 360 high drops the title, ≤ 440 wide narrows the captions).
- **Rules screen:** `Rules` (modal `SceneView`, text and tile pictures) plus the bar `RulesAction` (`Screen` canvas with Back / Next / Close; the buttons cannot be in the plate because 64 view units are 24 px in the smallest window). The page is clamped, 0 whenever closed; `FormatRulesPage` is published when the page, the open state or the language moved. The plate's blocks are its own children so a hidden one takes no room and `PageHeight` equals the real extent. A new match and any accepted control action close it.
- **End screens:** `HandOver` (modal `SceneView`, text only, **no dim**: all four hands stay face up and nothing of the plate covers a hand, meld or bonus tile, but in the landscape phone profile it still covers the viewer's melds and bonus tiles, which lie in a row at y 564..604: left for the owner; in portrait a variant of the plate, 504 wide, lines in two columns, between the side seats' columns and clear of the viewer's rows; narrow 856 wide) with `HandOverAction` (the `Next hand` / `Finish match` button, a `Screen` canvas over the plate's gap, in the middle of the window, in both shapes). The hand is settled for the screen with `SettleHand(Hand())`; scores move only on `NextHand`. More than 16 lines show 15 and `+n more`. **One hold:** when a person's claim lost to a mahjong (any winner) in a window the table component settled itself (a click or a pace step), `HandOver` and `HandOverAction` wait while `MahjongTableComponent::HandOverHeld()`, so the plate does not cover the flying tile and the marks; the words are published at once, and a window settled by a control client holds nothing. `MatchOver` (`Screen` canvas) shows the standings and its `New game` opens `Setup`. A top-notch window pushes the corner button onto the plate's top padding: accepted (owner's call).

## The hints (`MahjongHintsComponent`)

Apply when `HintsApply(HintsOn(), IsTablePaused(), session)`: hints on, not paused, `Playing`, the viewer a person, no cover. Never for a spectator or once the hand is over; the control API does not know them (`get_state` is equal with hints on and off).

- **Waiting row** in the top left corner (x 96..432, y 8..148, clear of the centre panel, which is the inside of the wall ring and which the hints leave free; the elements keep to the safe area, so a notch moves them inwards; in portrait a variant puts the row, the hint button and the advice in the free ground under the viewer's discards): the tiles the viewer's hand waits for with the unseen copies (`WaitingTiles`), while the hand has 3n+1 tiles. Recomputed only when the version, the viewer or `HintsApply` changed.
- **Declare marks:** gold bars under the hand tiles whose discard declares (`DeclaringDiscards`), on the viewer's turn.
- **Advice:** `HintButton` (node `HintsButton`, or in `ActionsCompact`) is offered while the viewer is the person to act. A press stores `AdviceFor(view)` for that table; the sentence goes to `hintAdvice` and the advised tile is framed. A second press while that advice is up opens the **reasons** (`ExplainAdviceFor`, `ReasonLines`: up to six lines, `hintWhy0..5`) in the panel `WhyPanel` of the node `HintsWhy` (layer 11), a third closes it. It lies on the wall ring, the one place with no hand, meld or discard tile, so while open it covers the centre panel; it is text on an opaque plate, takes no input and has no button of its own (the compact row is not touched). Any version change, viewer change, hints off, pause or cover drops the advice and the reasons with it. It never acts.
