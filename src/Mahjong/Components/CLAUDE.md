# Components/ — the scene

The glue between the model and the engine's scene and UI. Pure logic lives in `../Table/` (read its `CLAUDE.md`); the node files, the string table and the UI style are in `config/` (`config/CLAUDE.md`).

- `MahjongTableComponent` (node `Table`) draws the table and plays the person seats by mouse and buttons, paces the computers, flies tiles, shows the hot-seat cover, swaps `Actions` / `ActionsCompact`. After the layout it publishes `MakeTableRegions` to the canvas of every node in `kRegionNodes` (`SetRegion`, only what changed); those nodes anchor to the regions and do not move with insets.
- `MahjongHudComponent` (node `Hud`) publishes the HUD, seat-plate and centre-panel words and runs `Setup`, the rules screen and the end screens.
- `MahjongHintsComponent` (node `Hints`) runs the hints.
- `MahjongUploadComponent` (node `Consent`, layer 34): the glue of the tester upload (`../CLAUDE.md`, "Upload sink"). **Runs only where the page can post**, else nothing is done (no screen, no file). Its first frame loads `upload.txt` and then sets the uploader's sink, consent and the session's origin, in that order; the modal question is up exactly while the consent is `Unasked`; the destructor flushes the sink. It shows the rows of `Options` (below) and answers `MahjongSetUpload` (= `Choose`) and `MahjongNickname`. `TestOverrides()` is the test seam.
- `MahjongOnlineComponent` (node `Online`) shows the online panel (`OnlineScreens`, the canvases `Online` and `OnlineEntry`) and runs the client of `MahjongGame` once per frame; `Online/CLAUDE.md`, "The screens". Escape is the panel's while `IsOnlineOpen()` (the HUD ignores it then); the `Online` button of `Setup` hides with no address. Its pad also types the tester's nickname (`OpenNicknameEntry`, no page of `OnlineScreens`); the HUD leaves that Escape to it.
  - **Clipboard:** `OnlineCopy` or the lobby title writes the room code; `Paste`, `PasteEvent` fill it (`Online/CLAUDE.md`). A read comes late: its callback holds a `weak_ptr` of `_alive` and finds the component by `GetActive()`, never `this`.

## Rules for every component

- Subscribe with `SubscribeInScene` (mouse, `ActionEvent`, `SceneUpdateEvent`), so nothing reacts while another scene is active. Check `MahjongGame::GetActive()` and that a match is started.
- Change the game only through `TableSession` (`PerformPerson`, `StepComputer`, `NextHand`, `Reveal`). **The table component draws only from the model; hints never act** (no `PerformPerson`, no `Reveal`, no version change).
- A button handler asks again what is on offer *now* and ignores an event that is not offered (stale clicks, events after a control action).
- Publish `UIVars` only when something changed: the key is `TableSession::Version()` plus the version of `UIStrings` (a language switch rewords everything at the next draw). An unchanged frame must not move the `UIVars` version (tests check it).
- `IsTablePaused()` (`Setup`, `Options`, rules or the online panel open) stops everything on the table: clicks, action events, computer steps, the blink and flight clocks, the hints.
- Every word is a key (`text="{#key}"` in the scene, `Tr` in code).

## The table (`MahjongTableComponent`)

**Draw order:** felt (`Table/TableLook.h`), rim and its lip (also with no game; no box under the discards or the wall), the centre panel (ground, the turn light, the dealer mark), seat plates (each with its emblem and rings; the ground of a plate and of the panel is one rounded framed `UIBoxMesh` `DrawGeometry` into member scratch lists (`PanelScratchCapacity()`), the turn tint and frame in the same call; a mark bar is inset by the radius), the rack under the viewer's hand (`kHandRackLip` plate, `kHandRack` on it; public, wood, never gold), turn backdrop of `CurrentSeat()` (in a claim window the discarder, never `Actor()`), the highlight of a set just laid, calling frames, then every slot, then flying tiles in a second pass. A turned slot is drawn from `UprightRect(slot.rect)` about the centre of `slot.rect`; shadows and every mark are on the **screen** rectangle of the slot. `GetLayout()` is the layout of the last draw, built for the **active view** of the scene (one of four shapes by the aspect of the output, `Table/CLAUDE.md`) with the profile `TableProfileFor` gives the window; the rim, lip and shadows scale with `LayoutScale`; `NeedsCompactActions` and the tap regime read the view too.

**Table layout spec:** the zones come from the active `TableLayoutSpec`, which `OnAttached` fills from the config `TableLayout`. `OnDraw` polls the file stamp every `kTableLayoutPollSeconds` (wall clock, also paused or under a modal) and offers the cached document to `OfferTableLayoutSpec`, so an unsaved editor edit shows too. A spec that fails `CheckTableLayoutSpec` is refused, the last good one stays; a file that cannot be stat'ed (browser) is silent.

**No peeking:** while playing only the viewer's concealed tiles are face up; none under the hot-seat cover; all four once the hand is over **and while no seat is a person** (a watched match). The marks follow the viewer (`Faces::seat`), not the hands face up, so a spectator has none. Bottom seat = `BottomSeat(session)`.

**Marks** (all from public data; gold unless said otherwise, see `config/CLAUDE.md`): last discard tint; claimed-edge bar (`claimEdge`); drawn-tile tint (a face-up hand while playing); pending-discard frame (the viewer in `PendingClaimSeats()`); the **chow frame** on the two concealed tiles an offered chow takes (`ChowTilesTaken`: the hovered button's, else the only chow); the blink of a set just laid (after its tiles land); the calling frame of declared hands; the **centre panel** (a gold light on the edge facing `TurnSeat`, a red mark at the dealer's cell: `PanelTurnLight` / `PanelDealerMark`); the **wind bars** on the viewer's tiles of its seat wind (blue) or the round wind (violet; both: two halves), not gold, a face-up hand while playing (`WindMarkOf`); the keeps-waiting bars of the viewer's declared hand on its discard turn (**not tied to the Hints switch**); **a claim that lost**: after a window with a `beaten` claim (`LastClaimWindow()`, by its `serial`) the winner's plate blinks and every beaten seat's plate gets the `kClaimLostFrame` for `kClaimLostSeconds`.

**Flights:** each draw keeps a `TableSnapshot`; all moves since the previous draw are one batch planned by `PlanFlights`. A flight carries no face: the same `drawSlot` draws it at `FlightRect`, so a back flies as a back and the slot's marks travel with it. The face turns by the shortest way (`FlightPlacement`); the marks stay on the screen rectangle. A win is no move, so the snapshot carries it (`winSeat` / `winIndex`): a mahjong on a discard flies from its pile slot into the winner's hand. A new batch lands the running flights; a new hand / match / game (`StartCount()` too), another viewer, shape or view size start with none. `SetTileFlightSeconds(0)` turns them off (the test base does).

**Playing:** a person acts when `Playing`, the actor is a person, no cover, not paused.
- *Mouse:* a left click no canvas took, on a viewer's hand tile (`HitHandTile`) = `PerformPerson(MakeDiscard(tile, declareNext))`.
- *Hover name:* the pointer over a **face-up** tile (`TileOfSlot`, the draw's own rule; never a back, a wall stack or a tile in flight) words `tileHoverText` (`TileName`), on a viewer hand tile with a danger bar also `tileHoverDanger`, and shows the plate `TileHoverPlate` of the node `TileHover` below it (`HoverPlateRect`). The table **forgets** the pointer when paused, under the cover, on a touch or a pen, and while a canvas that takes input is modal or has a `hovered` element (`CanvasHasPointer`).
- *Tap regime* (`UsesTapToSelect`, `Table/TapSelect.h`): once a click SDL made from a touch or a pen was seen (`_touchSeen`, sticky), or while a hand tile is under `minTouchSize`, `StepTap` makes the first click on a tile a **selection** (drawn at `RaisedRect` after every other slot, its marks and the hints' declare bar and advice frame follow, `SelectedHandIndex()`); the same tile again discards, another moves it, a click on no tile clears it. The selection is kept as index + tile + turn + session version + hand, and dropped when any differs, in a claim window, under the cover, paused or with the regime off; the `Declare` toggle and a refused discard keep it. `selectedTileText` (`SelectedTileText`) names it, empty with none.
- *In a room* (`IsOnlineTable()`, `../CLAUDE.md`): every action goes through `Perform(game, action)`, online the first entry of the server's list (`OnlineTable::actions`) that equals it, sent by `OnlineClient::Act`; none listed (the view moved on) and the click is ignored with no word. A person acts when the client is in `Game` and the list is not empty; no computer is stepped, and the buttons, the declare veil, the chow frame and the keeps-waiting bars read that list (`LegalFor`), as a display hand lists nothing.
- *Buttons:* each frame `MakeActionButtons(LegalActions(actor))` → show/hide elements and set the tile pictures **by name** (so `Actions` and `ActionsCompact` both follow). Buttons emit `ActionEvent` (`MahjongWin`, `MahjongClaimPung`, `MahjongClaimChow` + index, `MahjongPass`, ...; the table is `SpecOf`).
- *Declare:* `MahjongDeclare` toggles "the next discard declares"; while on, non-declaring tiles are veiled and ignored; a discard, a frame where it is no longer offered, or a turn change clears it.
- *Pace:* one `StepComputer()` per `kComputerStepSeconds`; the player's speed (`GetAnimationSpeed()`) scales that, the flight time and the claim marks together (`Seconds()`); a claim window and the discard after it are one step. **A search seat thinks in the background** (`ComputerThinking`, `../CLAUDE.md`): the component begins the step (`BeginComputerStep`) at the start of the pause and applies it (`FinishComputerStep`) at the pause end, or when the answer arrives if the search is slower, never earlier; the step is dropped, nothing applied, when no computer acts any more, when `Version()` or `StartCount()` moved and with the component. With the manual clock the search is run to its end inside the frame.
- *Cover:* `Handoff` is shown exactly while `HandoffPending()`; its button calls `Reveal()`.

**Action buttons:** two canvases hold the same 13 buttons by name, and the component shows one (`NeedsCompactActions`, which counts the bottom inset; `TouchRegimeTest`). `Actions` is a `SceneView` canvas: `ActionRow` fills the region `table.actions`, the `Hint` label `table.hint`; the sizes per shape are `ActionButtonHeightFor` and the node's variants. `ActionsCompact` is a `Screen` canvas with `minScale` on the bar under the table, plus a `HintButton`; `Actions` and `HintsButton` hide then. Why two: the node file's header. `Actions` shows where its buttons are 32 px or more, or where the bar under the table cannot hold the compact row above the safe area (it would lie on the hand). The `Hint` label shows the sentence of the hovered button of `Actions` (none on the compact row).

## The HUD (`MahjongHudComponent`)

- **Words:** `lastMove` (`MoveText` of the last move; empty before the first move and once the hand is over; the `MoveLine` plate is shown exactly while it has text; the plate `SelectedTile` of the same node says `selectedTileText`, shown by the table component, not the HUD), and per screen position `p` `plateName<p>`, `panelWind<p>` (one letter), `panelScore<p>`, plus `panelRound` and `panelWall` (`MakeCenterPanel`). There is no turn word: the turn is the gold of a seat plate and the light of the centre panel.
- **Seat plates:** four plates of one size at the sides of the table (`zones.seatPlates`); the table component paints them (base, turn tint and frame, a red dealer bar, the emblem of the seat in `EmblemBox` with a turn ring and a calling ring), `SeatPlates` labels carry the name only, by fractions of the region `table.plate.<position>`, under the emblem. Public data: shown under the cover and at a hand's end.
- **Centre panel:** the table paints its ground, turn light and dealer mark (see Marks); the node `CenterPanel` words the round and hand (`East 1`), the wall and, on the side of each seat, its wind letter and points, by fractions of the region `table.centerPanel` (the inside of the wall ring); labels only, by screen position and upright for every side (no label turn in the engine).
- **In a room** (`IsOnlineTable()`): plates and sentences name the players (`SeatWho`, `Table/SeatName.h`); the button of `HudMenu` and of `MatchOver` reads `Leave room` (the variable `newGameWord`) and calls `OnlineClient::Leave()` once; the end of the table, not the click, opens `Setup`. `HandOverAction` sends the server's listed `next_hand` and is `disabled` while none is listed (sent, or the link is down); `HandCoachRow` says how many of four have read the plate (the hand tally is in the card); the score table pays the server's `result.scores` / `deltas` (a display hand lacks the first-hand calling bonus). `MoveLine` says the lost connection while `Reconnecting` and the client's own word (a view it could not read) until the table moves. A table that ended with a word (room gone, another device, another format) opens `Setup` and the online panel with that notice (`TakeRoomEndNotice()`); a `Leave` the player asked for says nothing.
- **`HudMenu`:** three round icon buttons of 56 (`Rules`, `Options`, `NewGame`; icon only, no word to cut) in a `Screen` canvas: a row at the window's top right corner with `NewGame` in it, its own variants (portrait, narrow, at most 440 units) putting them in the table's free ground. The tooltip is the word (`{#hud.rules}`, `{#hud.options}`, `{newGameWord}`). `MahjongRules` opens the pages from the table or from `Options` and is out of date under `Setup`, the online panel or the rules. It names no table region (its canvas scale differs from the table's fit). Hidden while the score table or the rules are up.
- **Save:** each frame (`SceneUpdateEvent`, active game only) the HUD calls `MahjongGame::FlushSave()` (`../Save/CLAUDE.md`).
- **`Setup`** (modal `Screen` canvas, a card): the group `SeatsGroup` of four seats, each with a level `UIDropdown` (`1` .. `10`, `MahjongSetSeatLevelN`) shown exactly while that seat is a computer in the pending setup, the group `MatchGroup` with hands 16 | 8 (a caption each), one row `Continue` (exactly while `CanContinue()`), `SetupOnline`, `Start` (flex weights). The component keeps the pending choices (taken from the game when the panel opens; a person's seat keeps its level) and applies them only on `Start` (`SetSeats`, `SetLevels`, `handsPerMatch` of a copy of the settings, `StartRandomMatch()`; a refused start puts all back). The close cross `SetupClose` (a round icon button) and Escape (`SDLEvents::KeyDownEvent`, not a repeat) call `CloseSetup()`: no start, no pending choice applied, the table behind the panel is where the player was. It never changes `flowers`, `limit` or `startingScore`.
- **`Options`** (modal `Screen` canvas, layer 31): language, hints off | hints | coach (Coach includes them), speed slow | normal | fast (`MahjongSetSpeed`, `speedChoice`: the pace, flights and claim marks), `Rules` and `Back`. The HUD button opens it unless `Setup` or the rules are up; `Back`, Escape and `CloseSetup()` close it. Its three actions (`MahjongSetHints` is `MahjongHintsComponent`'s) apply at once and only while it is open. Where the page can post, `MahjongUploadComponent` shows two more rows (`Send matches`, `Nickname`: a button that opens the pad) and grows the plate.
- **Rules screen** (from `HudMenu` or `Options`): `Rules` (modal `SceneView`, text and tile pictures) plus the bar `RulesAction` (`Screen` canvas, Back / Next / Close). The page is clamped (0 when closed); `FormatRulesPage` is published when the page, the open state or the language moved. A new match and any accepted control action close it. Line, item and pager text sits in a child (`RulesLineText<n>`, `RulesItemText<n>`, `PagerChip`): its margin is the air.
- **End screens:** `HandOver` (modal `SceneView`, **no dim**: all hands stay face up and the plate covers no hand tile, except the viewer's melds and bonus tiles in the landscape phone profile; percents of the view, four shapes) with `HandOverAction` (the `Next hand` / `Finish match` button, a `Screen` canvas over the plate's gap). Scores move only on `NextHand`. The seats are a **table** (`handOverCell<r>_<c>`: name, wind, hand, change, total; `-` for a drawn hand) with the winner's row lit (`WinnerRow<seat>`) and the **winning tiles** as pictures (`WinTile<N>`, the winning tile last and framed by `WinTileMark<N>`; public data, since all four hands are face up; in a room the points are the server's, the tiles the display hand's). More than 16 lines show 15 and `+n more`. **One hold:** when a person's claim lost to a mahjong in a window the table component settled itself, `HandOver` and `HandOverAction` wait while `MahjongTableComponent::HandOverHeld()` (the plate must not cover the flying tile and the marks); words are published at once; a control client's window holds nothing. `MatchOver` (`Screen` canvas) shows the standings as rows [place badge][name][score] (`standing<Place|Name|Score><n>`; ties share a place, `StandingFirst<n>` lit per first place); its `New game` opens `Setup`. In Coach mode, once the match tally has a move, each plate gets one tally row (`HandCoachRow`, `MatchCoachRow`, `FormatCoachTally`) inside the fixed plate heights (exactly full with it shown; `ButtonGap` stays at the view's middle, for the button).

## The hints (`MahjongHintsComponent`)

Apply when `HintsApply(HintsActive(), IsTablePaused(), session)`: mode Hints or Coach, not paused, `Playing`, the viewer a person, no cover. **In a room** the host decides (`OnlineTable::hintsAllowed`): `HintsActive()` / `CoachActive()` are Off unless the room allows them, else the player's own mode (`HintsOn()` / `GetHintsMode()` stay that choice: Options, the save); every hint is worked out from `MahjongHintsComponent::ViewOf` (the display hand, `legal` the server's `actions`), never from a hand but the viewer's. **The coach of a room** has a ledger of its own (`GetCoach()`, `CoachContext()`), fed in `Perform` with the view from before the player's action once `OnlineClient::Act` took it. Never for a spectator or once the hand is over.

- **Waiting row** in the free region `table.waiting`: the tiles the viewer's hand waits for with the unseen copies (`WaitingTiles`), while the hand has 3n+1 tiles.
- **Declare marks:** gold bars under the hand tiles whose discard declares (`DeclaringDiscards`), on the viewer's turn. **Danger marks** while an opponent has declared: a green / amber / red bar (safe / risky / dangerous, `DangerMarks`) inside the left edge, between the wind bar and the keeps-waiting bar.
- **Advice:** `HintButton` (node `HintsButton`, or in `ActionsCompact`) is offered while the viewer is the person to act, and is a **switch** (the component's state, kept across turns and matches, not saved). While on, every table the viewer acts on gets `AdviceFor(view)` by itself, once per table, and the advised tile is framed; in Hints the sentence is the label `AdviceText` (`hintAdvice`, top left). A version or viewer change, hints off, a pause or the cover drops it.
- **The card of the coach** (mode Coach): `CoachCard` of the node `HintsWhy` (layer 11) fills the wall ring over the centre panel and its turn light; no input (a press inside takes the advice down, read in `OnMouseDown`). It says the ledger's **note** while one is up (this hand and viewer, `MakeNoteCard`), else the **advice** while the switch is on (`MakeAdviceCard`), the tally last; in Coach `AdviceText` stays hidden. Published only when `CardKey` moved. The note goes on the next accepted action, a shown press, `Hint`, hand end, new match, pause, cover or a mode change.
