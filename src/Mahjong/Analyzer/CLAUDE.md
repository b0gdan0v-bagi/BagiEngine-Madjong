# Analyzer/ — the expected points of every legal action of one position

Namespace `BECore::Mahjong::Analyzer`, plain C++, no engine services. The program is `BagiMahjongAnalyzer` (`tools/analyzer/main.cpp` forwards `argv` and supplies the reader of files and of `-`); usage, the position text, how to read the columns and the measurement of the default: `docs/ev-analyzer.md`.

**A position is a `TableView` and nothing else.** Every entry point takes or returns a `TableView` (`MakeTableView(model, seat)`), never a `HandModel`, so "the analysis sees what the seat sees" holds by the types. A log position is replayed to the decision and reduced to the view of the acting seat; a hand-written position gets an arbitrary consistent fill for its hidden tiles and is reduced at once (the fill never reaches the analysis). A test of "no peeking" swaps hidden tiles between two models of one table and expects equal views and equal results.

| File | What |
|------|------|
| `AnalyzerRun` | `AnalyzePosition(view, config, loop)`: every element of `view.legal` is a candidate (a discard with and without `declareCalling` are two), played in the worlds and with the rollout seeds of the search player through `BeginSearchOver` (`Game/ComputerSearch.h`, the seam, so the draws cannot drift from the search); the value is `SettleHand(model).deltas[seat]`, Normal play on for all four seats. The unit of every interval is the **world** (`Arena::MeanCi` of per-world values or differences); the differences to the best row and to Normal's choice are paired by world. Normal's choice is the Normal chooser on the view with `kNormalChoiceSeed` (a tie is broken by that rng, so the seed is part of the report). The base seed is a function of `config.seed` alone; the values are summed in world order, so the result is the same for any loop and any `--jobs` |
| `AnalyzerPosition` | `PositionFromLog` / `PositionsOfSeat` (`hand` 1-based: hand K follows K-1 settlements; `actionIndex` 0-based among the `Action` events of the hand, passes of a claim window included), `ParsePosition` (the line grammar is in its header; `HandModel::Restore` judges what can exist) |
| `AnalyzerReport` | `FormatAnalysisReport` (fixed columns, `kReportPrecision` digits, no locale: the same bytes everywhere), `AnalysisToJson` / `FormatAnalysisJson` (`kAnalysisJsonVersion`; every number rounded to 0.01 before it is stored), `ActionNotation` (the one place that writes an action for a person), `PositionLabel` |
| `AnalyzerCli` | `ParseAnalyzerArgs`, `AnalyzerUsage`, `RunAnalyzerCli(args, readFile, output)`; exit codes 0 ok, 1 a position could not be read or analyzed, 2 bad arguments. The reader is a seam so a test needs no file; the first failure stops the run and the reports before it stay in `out` |

The numbers are the expected points against Normal bots (the opponents, and the seat itself after its action), with hidden tiles sampled uniformly: not an equilibrium. `vs best` has a small bias (the best row was picked by the same worlds). A new knob of the analysis must keep `Game/` free of JSON, threads and files and the output free of anything that differs between runs.
